Team page

The page a member of Payments lands on. One sentence says where the team stands, then three lists of rows: what needs this person, the projects with a status word each, and the latest handoffs. The lead sees one more list beneath, Memory housekeeping, which the curator writes: compactions it made, agent entries nobody read, conflicts waiting on a decision. Nothing is a card; the only accent on the page is the one button for the thing only this person can do.

As Dee, the lead

Dee opens Payments. The contention on services/tax/ is hers to decide, her own agent is blocked, and the curator has four housekeeping items for her, one of which is the memory conflict behind the contention.
