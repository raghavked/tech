Icon set

Twenty-eight line icons, one inline sprite. A 20px grid, a 1.5px stroke with round caps and joins, a 2px safe inset on every side. They inherit currentColor, so an icon is never a colour on its own: grey beside a tool line, ink in a button, and the accent only where the send arrow sits on it.

The set

Nouns first (where you are and who is here), then the verbs people do to a session, then the chrome every app needs. Fork and fold are a mirrored pair: two new ends, one way back.

Construction

The session glyph on its grid: the brand mark's turned corner, drawn as a line.

Grid
20 × 20 units, drawn at 20px; 1 unit = 1px
Stroke
1.5 units, round caps, round joins, no fills
Safe inset
2 units on every side; the apricot dash marks it
Corners
2.5 radius on sheets and squares, 1.5 on small boxes
Sizes
16 in rows and the sidebar, 20 in icon buttons, 24 in empty states, 14 in tool lines
Colour
currentColor only; set it on the row, never on the icon
Alignment
optical centre; the chevron sits 0.5 low, the send arrow 0.25 high

Sizes and turns

One drawing holds at every size. The chevron is one symbol turned by a class; its four directions are the only rotations in the set.
