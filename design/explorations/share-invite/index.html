Share a session, invite to a project

Sharing is a ten-second act, so it lives in a popover under the Share button, never a modal. Four things, top to bottom: the link, who can open it, a role per person, and a field to add someone. Roles are the kernel's lattice (observer, contributor, owner) said as words; the driver's seat is not a role here, it moves by handoff. Everything the popover changes is an event in the log, so the stream says what happened afterwards in the same voice as a tool step.

Irreversible calls need two owners or drivers. Right now that is you and Dee. Every change here is an event in the session log.

Link first. The address is the thing most people came for; it is a read-only field with the one accent button beside it. The same link opens the desktop app when it is installed.

Who can open it is three radio rows, not a dropdown. The two wider scopes name the role they grant, and it is always observer: a link never hands out the ability to steer.

A role per person is a word with a chevron, boxed only on hover. Roles that follow from the project (Dee is lead, so owner) are plain words with a reason, and have no remove button. The driver is shown as presence, "here · driving", not as a role: the seat moves by handoff, not from this popover.

Pending is a dashed avatar and the word "invited" with a warm dot. Removing a pending person withdraws the invitation; the button says so.

The footnote ties roles to the approval rule that matters this week: who can approve delete legacy_invoices. It changes as roles change.

Invitations are project events; Dee sees them on the project page. A lead invite needs Dee to confirm.

Same shape as Share. Head, a field, radio rows, one accent button. Someone who has shared a session already knows how to invite to a project.

Roles explained by consequence. "Member · contributor on every session" says what the word will mean next Tuesday when they open someone else's session. The default is member.

The join link is secondary. Projects are where claims and the lead's direction live, so a link that anyone can forward is the quiet button here, with its expiry beside it, rather than the first thing.

The optional line is prefilled with the lead's current direction, "schema freeze until Thursday", because that is the one thing a new member needs before their first turn.
