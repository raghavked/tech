Three sidebars for the desktop app

One agent, many humans. The sidebar is where Ana finds her own sessions, the fleet's, and the project they belong to. Three shapes of the same list: nested under projects, flat by recency, or folded into a rail. Hover a row to see the hover state; the active row is Ana's session.

What to carry forward

RecommendedA, projects with nested sessions. Fold's sidebar lists other people's agents, not only your own chats; the project is the thing they share, so it has to be visible without a switcher.
Borrow from BThe recency groups for a project with many closed sessions, inside the project's own page rather than the sidebar.
Borrow from CThe collapse: A folds to C's rail with one keystroke, and the project crumb appears in the top bar when it does.
