The approval notice, in every state

An approval is the one place a session stops and asks a person. The notice is the only bordered box in the stream, so it has to be the quietest one: a question in a sentence, the state in words with a dot, two buttons. Once answered it stops being a box and becomes a line, like any tool step. Irreversible and exec differ only in words, never in colour.
