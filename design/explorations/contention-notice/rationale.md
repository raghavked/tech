# Contention notice: rationale

**Decision.** A contention is the one box in the stream where the team, not the agent, is at odds, so its hairline is the apricot crease rather than the grey line an approval uses; no new hue appears. Inside it is rows and words: the question in a sentence, one row per side quoting the directive verbatim with the author's name in the serif, a small plain Pick at the end of the driver's rows, and a status line with the apricot dot ("Scope tax frozen · you decide"). Neither Pick is primary: the accent stays on the send arrow because the product must never lean toward a side. An author's row swaps Pick for a quiet Withdraw; an observer sees no buttons. The agent's own line, "Scope tax is under discussion", precedes the box. Picked, replaced and withdrawn each collapse to one grey line quoting what now stands. A contention on `goal` keeps the same box and only changes the status words to "Session blocked".

**Rejected.** Side-by-side columns for the two steers (a card pair). A primary button on the later or driver-favoured steer (a nudge). A red treatment for blocked goal contentions (words carry it).

**Carry forward.** Verbatim rows with a row-end action, no primary when choices are symmetric, box-to-line on settlement, and "needs you" as the single imperative row word across approvals and contentions.
