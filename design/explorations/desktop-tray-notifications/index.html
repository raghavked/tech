The tray and the native notification

Desktop Fold is a Tauri shell around the same client. Two things only it can do: speak through the operating system's own notifications, and sit in the menu bar or system tray while the window is closed. Both carry the fewest words that let the person decide whether to come back: the ask as a sentence, who is asking, where, and a verb.

Cast: Northwind › Payments › Billing page › Invoice PDF + tax lines. Ana is driving; the agent is Ana's agent; Dee leads. The notifications below are what Bo sees when Ana hands off, and what a second driver sees when a deletion needs quorum.

Top-right banners in the system style: the app icon, the ask as the title, one line of body, and the actions that appear when the banner is hovered or expanded.

Hovered banner above, resting banner below. The icon is the mark on Dress Blues; the menu bar item shows the mark and the count of things that need this person.

The title is the ask. Not "Approval requested" but the question itself, with the path or the session name in it, so the person can decide from the banner alone.

Irreversible is never one tap. A quorum approval offers Review and Deny. Review deep-links to the notice in the session; Deny is safe because the agent will ask again with more context. A reversible call would offer Approve and Deny inline.

Handoff has no buttons. Clicking it opens the brief. Driving is taken in the session, where the directive and the brief are in front of you.

The same two sentences, set in the Windows shape. The app line names Fold once; the title does not repeat it.

Same words, different chrome. The copy is written once per event in the kernel and rendered by each OS. Nothing is designed that one platform cannot show.

No counts in the title. "2 approvals pending" belongs to the tray; a toast is one event with one verb.

Dismiss is not Deny. The close cross only removes the toast. The approval stays open in the session and in the tray count.

What every notification says

One shape for all events, so the words are written once and read the same way in the inbox, the tray and the OS.

Title
The ask as a sentence, with the object in it. Approve delete legacy_invoices? · Ana handed you Invoice PDF + tax lines
Body
Who is asking and where, then the one fact that changes the answer: Ana approved, needs 1 more driver · schema freeze until Thursday
Actions
At most two, naming the act. Irreversible calls get Review and Deny; reversible ones get Approve and Deny; handoffs and contentions get none and open the session.
Deep link
fold://p/northwind/s/invoice-pdf-tax-lines#ev_8c2d, straight to the notice in the stream, replayed from the log if the window was closed.
When
Only when the person is not looking at that session, and only for things that need them: approvals they can answer, handoffs to them, a directive from the lead that changes their course. Never for tool steps or progress.
Counted
The tray and the sidebar show one number: things that need this person. Someone else's approval, a contention they are not party to, and done items are listed but never counted.
