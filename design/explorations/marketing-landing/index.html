Session

Everyone opens the same run.

Not a read-only link, not a Slack thread the model has to make sense of. The session is the shared object: the driver steers, peers suggest, and when two people steer at once the kernel composes their directives by rule instead of by arrival order.

Irreversible tool calls wait for a quorum of distinct humans. Branches fork at a checkpoint and fold back with a three-way merge. A handoff gives the next driver a brief computed from the log.

Fleet

A project holds every engineer's agent.

Sessions claim the paths, services and tickets they are on; a write into another engineer's claim is refused as a tool result the agent can read. A lead's direction reaches every session in the project, above owner rank.

Where two sessions pull against each other, both owners and the lead see one contention, and the agents work around it until someone decides.

Priced for dropping in.

Per-seat pricing fights a product whose premise is that anyone on the team can join a session. So watching and contributing cost nothing. You pay for the agent's time and for the people who can approve.

Session-hours
Per active hour. Time spent waiting on humans is not billed.

Per person with driver or owner rights.

Per harness adapter and retained-log volume, for teams running Fold as their session layer.
