# Handoff flow: rationale

**Decision.** A handoff is an offer, a brief and an answer, drawn with shapes the stream already has. The offer and the receipt are quiet notices: Ana picks a person from rows with presence and writes one line of why; Bo sees it above the brief, laid out as five rows in I-PASS order (situation, open, recent turns with "new to you", changes since Bo's last turn, workspace), and restates the plan in one line before Accept does anything. Acceptance is the one event that changes authority, so it is drawn as a divider line, "Bo took the seat from Ana · 17:02 · turn 10", with the apricot ring moving from A to B in the top bar, the sidebar and the row by-line; Bo's restatement follows as the first human bubble. A decline is a grey line with the reason and one link, "offer to Cy". While an offer is pending the accent sits on its button and the send arrow steps back to ink.

**Rejected.** A modal or drawer for the brief (it belongs in the column). A banner for the transfer (the divider says it, the ring shows it). A "driver changed" status word (status words stay about the agent). Cards per brief section.

**Carry forward.** The I-PASS rows as the brief component everywhere; the receiver's line as a required field; the divider as the only drawing of an authority change; "offered" as the row word for a pending seat, beside "needs you".
