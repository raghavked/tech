Session rows, every state

A session row has to say four things without becoming a card: what the agent is doing, whether something changed since you looked, whether it is waiting on you, and whether someone stopped it. Status lives in a six-pixel dot and a word. Unread is weight. Needs you is one small word at the end. A pause ordered by project direction is the only dot that changes shape.

In the sidebar

Ana is signed in. Recents is a plain list of titles; the dot carries the status, the title's weight carries unread, and the only trailing marks are a word for "you" and a dot for "new". Nothing on the right edge counts anything.

The three markers

Each row has one status dot on the left and at most one mark on the right. When two apply, the right slot shows the more urgent and the other survives in the title or the dot.

Tax rounding audit

Unread

The agent wrote, or a person steered, since you last reached the end. Weight 500 on the title and a five-pixel ink dot on the right. Not a count: a session is a conversation, not an inbox.

Invoice PDF + tax linesyou

Needs you

An approval, a handoff to confirm, or a contention you decide. Amber dot and the word you. It outranks unread in the right slot. Only on sessions where you are a driver or the lead; Bo's pending approval is not Ana's amber.

Refund ledger

Paused by direction

A lead's project directive stopped it, so no one on the session can resume. The dot becomes two bars in the same grey. A pause by a person stays a round dot; the difference is who can undo it.

Precedence in the right slot: you, then the new dot, then nothing. In the left dot: amber, red, green, grey, two bars, faded, hollow, none. The open session gets the apricot bar and nothing else changes.

The row itself

Rest, pointer, open and keyboard. Hover is one tint; open is the page colour with the apricot bar; focus is the ring, kept inside the row so the list never shifts.
