Composer variants

One sheet with a folded corner, the same in every state. The mode, the scope, the audience and the interrupt live as words inside it; the only colour is the arrow. Below, the composer at the bottom of Northwind › Payments › Billing page › Invoice PDF + tax lines, with Ana driving.

Nothing typed, nothing chosen

2 · Expanded, mode popover open

The plus opens five words

Typed text grows the sheet to eight lines. The plus (or a leading slash) opens the directive modes as rows: the one in use is marked, each has a one-line meaning and the authority it needs. Resume and cancel are greyed for a contributor; Ana is driver so all five are live.

3 · Scope chip editing

The scope is a word you can type

Clicking the chip turns it into a short field. The scopes this session already knows appear beneath as plain words: the contended one is struck through because it is frozen until the team settles it, and a new word simply becomes a new scope. Here Ana is constraining, so the chip reads constrain and the hint changes with it.

4 · Interrupt now

A checkbox, not a red button

While the agent is mid-turn the composer shows what it is doing on a line above the text and offers interrupt now as a checkbox beside the chips. Checked, the hairline turns apricot (a human's attention is here) and Enter stops the agent after the running tool call. Unchecked, the directive waits for the turn to end.

6 · The keyboard hint line

One line under the sheet, always the same place

Twelve and a half pixels, secondary grey, centred. It never explains the interface; it names the two or three keys that matter in the state the composer is in, so nobody reads a shortcut sheet. On the phone it is dropped.
