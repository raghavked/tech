The details drawer, as five plain lists

Everything the log knows about a session that is not the conversation itself: what the agent is meant to do, who is here, which branches exist, what the team remembers, and what changed while you were away. It opens beside the column on a wide screen and rises as a bottom sheet on a phone. Both are the same five lists; nothing is a card, every row carries its author on the right, and the only colour is the crease.

Full brief

Open. The sheet takes most of the height; the lists scroll inside it, the column stays where it was.

Same lists, same words. The phone sheet is the desktop drawer at full width. Rows that carried two lines on desktop carry one here; the author moves into the right-hand meta or drops, never into a second font.

No tabs. Five short lists fit one sheet; a tab bar would hide four of them and add chrome. The sheet scrolls, and the section labels are enough to find a place.

Status is a word with a dot. Here, Away, Waiting, Contention, Conflict. Contention and the branch conflict also get the apricot rule on the left, the same mark the notice uses in the column, so the drawer and the stream agree.

One accent. The send arrow is the only filled control on either screen. Drawer buttons are quiet text: Pause, Fork, Resolve, Mark read.
