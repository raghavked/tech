# Details drawer: rationale

**Decision.** The drawer is five plain lists under five small grey labels: Intent, People, Branches, Memory, Catch-up. Every row is one line with its author or count in grey on the right, so it reads like the log it is computed from. Intent is the lattice itself, one row per component (goal, direction, constraint, steer, control), with the contended scope marked by the same apricot rule the contention notice uses in the column. People is a name, a role word and a presence word with a dot; the pending handoff is an indented row under Bo. Memory keeps engineer, session and commit on every line and shows a conflict as two rows against each other. Catch-up is the computed brief in three sentences. On a phone the identical lists rise as a bottom sheet over a dimmed column, same order and words. The only filled control on either screen is the send arrow.

**Rejected.** Tabs across the five sections (hides four, adds chrome). Avatars and pills for people and status (badges where a word and a dot do). A card per memory entry or branch (a dashboard, not a drawer). A mobile summary bar above the composer (competes with the one input).

**Carry forward.** The row grammar `label · value | meta`, the apricot left rule shared by contention and conflict in drawer and stream, the indented sub-row for a pending thing, and the bottom sheet as the drawer unchanged.
