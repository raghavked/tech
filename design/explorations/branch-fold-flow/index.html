Fork, compare, fold

A branch is a pointer to a turn plus what happened after it. It costs nothing to make, so forking should be as light as replying: one action under a turn, one line of why. Compare is the only time the column splits, and it splits into two of the same column. Folding back is a quiet notice that lists files as rows; the one conflict is the row that opens, with both sides and a choice. When it settles, the whole thing becomes a line. No branch chrome, no graph, no diff viewer: the stream, twice.

2Compare side by side
