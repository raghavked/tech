Microcopy

Every string the product says, in one voice: calm, specific, naming the person and the act. The ten rules first, then each surface as a table, each row with the phrasing it replaces. The web app keeps these in apps/web/src/copy.ts; the Slack adapter and the shells say the same words.

Cast throughout: Northwind › Payments › Billing page › “Invoice PDF + tax lines”. Ana drives, Bo and Cy contribute, Dee leads. The agent is Ana’s agent.

1Ten rules

The voice is a small grammar. Everything below follows from it.

01
Name the person and the act.Ana is driving. Bo joined as contributor. not: A user has joined the session.
02
Say the thing, not the technology.The agent wants to run pnpm vitest. not: Your AI assistant would like to execute a command.
03
Status is one of five words.running · awaiting approval · blocked · paused · idle, with a dot, never a pill, the same in the sidebar, the fleet and Slack. not: In progress, Pending review, Error.
04
Present tense for state, past tense for the log.Nobody else here yet. Share the link. · Folded pdf-layout into main. not: The branch has been successfully merged.
05
Verbs on buttons, one or two words.Approve · Deny · Pick · Withdraw · Hand off · Fold into main. not: Submit, OK, Yes, Confirm, Got it.
06
Fold’s own words for Fold’s own ideas.A session is the fold; a person drives it; handing over offers the fold; a merge folds a branch in; steers and constraints are directions; two at once is a contention. not: ownership transfer, merge request, conflict of instructions.
07
Quiet errors: what failed, where, and the next move.Could not load /api/me: 503. not: Oops, something went wrong.
08
Middle dots separate facts on a line.Ana · contributor · away. Sentences end with a full stop; labels and buttons do not. not: Ana, contributor (away)
09
Count, then noun.1 approval waiting · 3 conflicts · 2 of 2 drivers. not: You have three (3) new conflicts.
10
No emoji, no exclamation marks, no apology from the model.An ellipsis only while something is in progress: Joining… · Loading…. not: Done! · Sorry, I can’t do that. · any emoji

2Statuses

Five words for an agent, three more for a session or a client. A word and a dot; the dot is the only colour.

WordMeansNot this
runningThe agent is inside a turn. The sidebar dot pulses; the row reads what it is doing: Writing packages/billing/pdf.ts.In progress · Working on it
awaiting approvalA tool call waits on a person. Counted as 3 awaiting approval; in a title, 1 approval waiting.Pending · Needs review
blockedA write was refused, a scope is held by a contention, or a step failed and the agent cannot pass. Always followed by why: blocked · write to schema.ts refused: held by Cy’s agent.Error · Stuck · Failed
pausedA person paused it. Say who: paused by Dee.On hold · Suspended
idleThe turn ended and nothing is queued. The next directive starts it.Waiting for input · Ready
cancelledThe driver cancelled the turn. The log stays readable.Aborted · Killed
closedThe session is finished and folded; the fleet no longer counts it.Archived · Completed
offlineThis client lost the server, not the agent. Offline · last synced event 4 minutes ago.Connection lost! · Disconnected
