Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? anywhere in the desktop or web app and the whole map appears on one sheet: four groups, one row per shortcut, the keys on the right. The sheet knows what is live in the session you are looking at, so "Approve what is waiting" names the delete and the fold row says why it is off. Keys follow the platform; nothing is a card.

Four groups, in the order of a day. Session is what you do to the thing; Steering is talking to the agent; Approvals is what it asks of you; Navigation is getting around. Nothing is listed twice and nothing is listed that has no key.

A row is words, then keys. The verb on the left in ink, the keys on the right as soft keycaps with no border. Context in grey after a middle dot: the turn you would fork from, who the handoff goes to, what the pending call is.

The sheet is live. A row that cannot run now stays on the map but goes grey and says why: "schema freeze until Thursday" is Dee's direction, not a disabled state. The approval row names the call so a lead never approves blind.

Single letters only when the composer is empty. A, D, J, K, O and G chords behave like a mail client; the moment there is text in the composer they type. No letter is ever destructive on its own: A on an irreversible call still needs the second driver.

Platform is a word, not a detection. ⌘ and Ctrl are both in the markup; a radio in the sheet picks one, and the app presets it from the shell. The one accent stays on the send arrow, which turns to ink while the sheet is open.
