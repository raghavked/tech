Keyboard first

The cursor is a row, not a button. Keys act on the whole row, so a lead can clear a morning's queue without the mouse; tabbing still reaches every real button.

jkMove the cursor down or up. It skips project headers and the decided lines.
aApprove the cursor row. For an irreversible call the row asks once more in place: Approve delete? a again, or any other key.
dDeny. The row turns into a decided line and the cursor moves to the next row that needs you.
↵Open the session at the turn that raised the call, with the notice in view.
gJump to the next project header.
123Need me, All, Decided. The filter is the segmented control in the top bar.

What the row never shows. Avatars, risk badges, coloured borders, or a progress pair for the quorum. The count "1 of 2 drivers" and the words "cannot be undone" do that work. The sidebar carries one trailing phrase, "3 need you", and nothing else.
