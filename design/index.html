The studio where your team and its agents work on one piece.

Many hands, one piece. Three strokes, one apex. The mark is the whole idea: several people and several agents steering one long-running session, and converging.

  • Apricot stroke: the agent, warm, in the middle of it
  • Navy stroke: the team and the chrome around the work
