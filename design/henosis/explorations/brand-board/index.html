02 / EVERY SIZE

From 512 to 16

Two cuts. The default cut from 32 px up; the small cut, with a heavier ring and bigger bead, at 24 px and below.

Default cut above 32, small cut (·) at 24 and belowalways on whole pixels

Why the small cut exists. At 16 px the default ring is 1.7 px wide and the bead is 2.5 px: the gap closes and the mark reads as a plain circle. The small cut keeps the ring at 2.3 px and the bead at 3.3 px, so the opening and the bead both survive. Left: default; right: small.

Where each size lives. 512 app store · 192 and 128 onboarding and the join screen · 96 project page and empty states · 48 the rail brand and desktop titlebar · 32 avatars for the Henosis system account · 24 tray and tab icons · 16 favicon and inline mentions.

05 / CLEAR SPACE

One bead of air

The unit is b, the bead's outer diameter (15 of 96). Nothing enters the box drawn at 1b from the mark's edge, and the wordmark is set 2b from the disc.

Clear space and the lockupb = 15 / 96 of the mark's width
  • 01
    One bead on every side. Text, other logos, the edge of a card or a window: all stay outside the dashed box.
  • 02
    Two beads to the wordmark. The horizontal lockup uses 2b between disc and the h; the stacked lockup uses 1b between disc and the x-height.
  • 03
    Baseline on the disc. The wordmark's baseline sits 0.22 of the disc below its centre, which lines the x-height with the ring's centre.
  • 04
    In the product, clear space is the padding. The rail brand row is 48 px high for a 24 px mark: that is 1b of air at 24 px, rounded up to the grid.
  • 05
    Avatars are the exception. The system account's 28 px avatar carries the mark at 20 px with the disc as the avatar fill. Nothing else may crowd the ring.
06 / MISUSE

What the mark is not

Each of these breaks the one idea: a ring open at the top, completed by a chocolate bead.

×
Don't rotate itThe gap is at 12 o'clock. A gap elsewhere is a different mark.
×
Don't close the ringThe opening is the invitation; without it there is nothing to join.
×
Don't drop the beadThe ring alone is a loading spinner. The bead is the newcomer.
×
Don't move the beadIt sits in the gap, on the ring's path, nowhere else.
×
Don't recolour itApricot ring, chocolate bead. Team accents change the glow, not the mark.
×
Don't stretch itScale from the corner. The ring is a circle, always.
×
Don't gradient the ringGradients are for hero moments and the new-session button, not the mark.
×
Don't drop the disc on a busy groundBare ring only on flat navy or near-black. Everywhere else, keep the disc.
