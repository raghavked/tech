02 / EVERY SIZE

From 512 to 16

Three cuts. The default from 32 px up; a small cut with thicker arcs from 20 to 31; below 20 the disc goes and the ground becomes the disc.

Default from 32, small cut (·) 20 – 31, tiny cut (··) below 20always on whole pixels, never scaled by the browser

Why the cuts exist. At 16 px the default arcs are 1.7 px wide, the centre is 2.3 px and the disc leaves a 2 px navy halo: the mark is a dark blob with a dot. The small cut takes the disc to the edge and the arcs to 13 units (3.3 px at 24). The tiny cut drops the disc and lets the arcs grow to 16 units, 2.7 px at 16, with a 3.3 px centre. Left to right, on navy and on cream: default, small, tiny.

Where each size lives. 512 app store · 256 and 128 onboarding and the join screen · 96 project page and empty states · 48 the join notice · 24 the rail brand, tray and tab icons · 20 the system avatar · 16 favicon and inline mentions.

04 / APP ICON

Navy, cream, apricot, chocolate

Four grounds, each one of the palette, so the ground is the disc. Navy is the store icon. Cream is for docs and print. Apricot and chocolate are the Team look editor's choices.

Navy: defaultchocolate + apricot
Cream: docs, printchocolate + navy
Apricot: team lookchocolate + navy, cream centre
Chocolate: team lookapricot + navy
Platform masksthe ring is never clipped: 18 units of clearance inside every shape
Rules of the ground The colour of each arc follows the ground, not the theme: the person's arc stays chocolate on everything but chocolate, where it becomes apricot; the agent's arc is apricot on navy and navy everywhere else. The centre is always the lightest colour present. On a ground that is not one of the four colours, keep the disc.
05 / CLEAR SPACE

One arc width of air

The unit is s, the arc's stroke (10 of 96). Nothing enters the box drawn at 1s from the disc, and the wordmark sits 3s from it.

Clear space and the lockups = 10 / 96 of the mark's width
  • 01
    One arc on every side. Text, other logos, the edge of a card or a window: all stay outside the dashed box, 1s from the disc.
  • 02
    Three arcs to the wordmark. The horizontal lockup uses 3s (.3em) between disc and h; the stacked lockup uses 3s between disc and the ascender line.
  • 03
    Centre on the x-height. The mark's centre, the dot, sits on the centre line of the wordmark's x-height. The mark is as tall as the wordmark's em.
  • 04
    In the product, clear space is the padding. The rail brand row is 48 px high for a 24 px mark: 12 px above and below is more than 1s, rounded to the grid.
  • 05
    Avatars are the exception. The system account's 28 px avatar carries the small cut at 20 px with the disc as the avatar fill. Nothing else may crowd the ring.
  • 06
    Bare marks need the same air. With the disc dropped, measure s from where the disc would have been (r 46), not from the ring.
06 / MISUSE

What the mark is not

Each of these breaks the one idea: two arcs of two colours, closing into one ring around one centre.

×
Not one colour for both arcsThen it is a ring with a dot. The two colours are the two members.
×
Not without the centreThe ring alone is a loading spinner. The centre is the work.
×
Not rotatedThe seams are at 12 and 6. Person right, agent left, always.
×
Not off-paletteTeam accents change the glow and the selection, never the arcs.
×
Not gradient arcsTwo flat colours with a seam. Gradients are for hero moments.
×
Not bare on a busy groundPhotos, gradients, patterns: keep the disc so the arcs have a room.
×
Not stretchedScale from the corner. The ring is a circle, always.
×
Not swappedThe person's arc is the right arc. Mirrored, it is someone else's mark.
