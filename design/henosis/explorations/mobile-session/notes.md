# Mobile session · notes

## The idea
On a phone the rail folds flat into the navy top block: the title, the running status, who drives, the Team pill "Team · with Bo", the token meter and the crew all sit in 110px, so the frame is still Dress Blues and the page is still cream. The stream is anchored to the latest moment, so the release gate that needs you is the last thing above the composer, and its stars are five thumb-sized tiles with the default baked into the button ("Approve with 4"). The composer keeps the Agent/Team toggle and the scope chip at the thumb, with one send button, and the plan collapses to a one-line ledger (steps as dots, used against estimate) that opens the full card.

## What to keep
- Navy top block = rail + title row + strip; the rail opens from the menu button with the needs-you count (3) in apricot, the only live colour in the frame.
- The strip: Team pill, mono "12.4k / 20k" with a hairline budget, crew in serif apricot.
- The release gate on a phone: irreversible chip, Bo's vote with stars and note in one line, 48px star tiles (filled up to the pick, the pick lit apricot with the glow), the note field, 1-of-2 progress with two gate segments, and a full-width primary "Approve with 4" beside a quiet "Deny".
- The plan line: dots for steps (green done, apricot now, grey to do) and "11.9k of 13.2k est." so the ledger survives at 390px.
- Team messages keep the apricot wash and "to the team" in italic serif; human bubbles go full width and carry the scope chip inline.
- Stream anchored to the bottom (column-reverse) so the newest and the pending gate are visible on open.

## Open questions
- Should the release gate be a bottom sheet that rises over the stream (it already looks like one) so it survives scrolling, or stay inline and get a "1 waiting" chip in the top block when it scrolls away?
- The strip hides presence (Ana, Bo, Cy are only in the Team button); is "Ana drives" in the subtitle enough, or should the avatars live in the strip and the Team button be just a glyph?
- Tapping a star on a phone: pick on tap, or tap-and-hold to show the 1-5 labels ("risky" to "ship it") for people who have never rated?
- The composer's scope chip (goal) and mode (steer/constrain) need a sheet on mobile; this page shows only the chip.
- Push notification deep link: land on the gate (as here) or on the handoff brief when you were not here for the last hour?
