# Mobile rail drawer and Team sheet · notes

## The idea
On a phone the frame unfolds rather than changes: the navy top block is the rail folded flat, and the menu button swings the same navy in from the left as a 332px drawer, so opening the rail reads as the frame opening, with the session still showing 58px at the right under the scrim. The Team panel rises from the bottom as a cream sheet with two detents, half (424px) for a glance at who is here and what needs you, full (96px from the top) for the room: people, the crew with its navy crewmate tile and claims, and the chat with its input pinned under the team glow. Apricot keeps its one meaning in both: solid for what waits for you (the menu's 3, Inbox 2, Approvals 1, #billing 2), a wash for where you are (the active card, the handoff offer), and chocolate stays the hand (driving, Accept, Team up).

## What to keep
- The drawer order, the same as the desktop rail: brand with the mark and a close X; a "who · where" line (Ana · Payments, with the project switcher and "3 agents · 2 chats"); New session on the one gradient; Search with its hint; Inbox and Approvals with counts; agents by crew (crew name in serif apricot, Solo as a pill); Memory with its conflict as an amber hint; Chats with unread; New group.
- 44px rows, 76px agent cards with one line of "what it is on", counts as mono chocolate-on-apricot 20px discs, spend past 80 % of the budget warming to amber (17.1k on Checkout proration).
- The you row pinned at the bottom with a hairline above and the list fading under it; the account menu rising on rail-2 from the three dots: theme segment, notifications and calm mode as switches, Team look (with the team name), shortcuts, sign out in the danger red.
- The sheet head mirrors the desktop drawer head: handle, "Team" with the live dot and "2 here · 1 away", the Team | Details segment; the same panel state drives both.
- People rows: serif names with "you" small, presence as a dot on the avatar, the driver's chocolate flag, "Hand off" only on the row that can take the baton, away rows dimmed with "away 12m".
- The handoff offer on the apricot wash at the top of the half sheet, Accept primary and Decline quiet, with the one sentence about what accepting means.
- In the full sheet: the crewmate tile in navy exactly like the rail card, the claims list with the one held by Cy's agent in warn, "Cy joins" as an italic divider, "Bo is writing to the team" with three apricot dots, and "Kept in the session log. Never sent to the agent." under the pinned input.
- Dark mode from the tokens alone; the scrim deepens to black at 58 %.

## Open questions
- Should the drawer close on a route change (as the app does) or stay open when the person taps a second agent card, so switching between two sessions is one gesture instead of three?
- The full sheet is 24px short of holding "Working on Invoice rollout with Cy · Leave crew"; either the crew section folds its claims by default, or Leave crew moves into the crewmate tile's overflow.
- With four or more people the half sheet becomes a list; the offer card should then stay pinned and the rows scroll under it.
- The keyboard pushes the sheet up: the chat input stays, but does the people list collapse to a stack of avatars so the last three messages remain visible?
- Swipe from the left edge opens the drawer on iOS; on Android that gesture is "back". Should the drawer answer only to the menu button there?
- The Details face of the sheet (intent, branches, usage) is not drawn here; the segment implies it has the same two detents.
