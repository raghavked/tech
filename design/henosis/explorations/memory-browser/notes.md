# Memory browser · notes

## The idea
Team memory is one page that answers "what does Payments believe, who said so, and where do we disagree": a scope segment (Org 118 / Team 41 / Project 17) with a picker and a search field, then conflicts before anything else as a two-sided notice with "Keep this" under each side and the line "nothing reads either until someone keeps one", then every entry as an attributed row (key in mono, the sentence, "added by Bo · session invoice-pdf-3 · commit 0c8d44 · Billing page") with its kind on the right, and the curator's report last as a quiet list of compacted, stale, conflict and proposed rows. The aside keeps the counts, who remembers (people in apricot, agents in the brand gradient, "each one is signed by the person who was driving") and the scope tree with the open count beside Payments. States are real: an open conflict that blocks Tax lines, a conflict Bo just resolved with the loser struck through and the reason in serif italics, a retracted row greyed with Ana's reason, a superseded row dimmed, the keyboard cursor on a row with its own hint, and a stale agent entry waiting for a person to say "Still true".

## What to keep
- Conflicts first, both sides side by side with the quote in Instrument Serif, the session, commit and scope under it, "read by 3 sessions" beside each Keep, and the foot that says both can be wrong and offers "Write the rule yourself".
- The resolved state folds into two lines (kept with a drawn check, retracted by Bo struck through) with the reason in the header; it is the same card, calmer.
- One attribution grammar everywhere: added by <name> · session · commit · scope, name in serif; the curator's summaries say "folded by the curator from 6 entries · Ana, Bo and Ana's agent" so a summary is never anonymous.
- Kind as a small circle: check for decision, lines for rule, i for fact, gradient disc for summary; retracted strikes the key and the sentence, superseded only dims.
- The curator never retracts: its proposals carry Retract/Keep for a person, and stale rows carry Still true/Retract.
- Mobile: the search goes to the top of the tools, the sides stack, the aside hides, the word and age sit under the row.

## Open questions
- Should Keep this need the driver or owner role, or anyone at contributor? The spec says contentions are the lead's; memory conflicts have no stated role.
- The aside counts (read in the last hour, oldest still read) need a read log per entry; the API has reads per conflict side only today.
- History on: does it show retracted entries inline in order (as drawn) or in a separate folded group, and do resolved conflicts stay at the top or drop into the entries list?
- "Remember" in the topbar: a sheet with key, sentence, scope and kind, or a line in the session composer that the agent sees too?
- Project scope hides the scope word unless an entry has a path; is "Billing page · invoice.pdf" the right way to show a path, or should the path be mono?
