Menu bar · the item is the ring and two numbers (decisions, running); the panel is the desktop tray's menu made rich: buttons on rows, dots that live, the meter
Spec
RefreshNeeds you on push (an approval opens or closes); Agents and Tokens every 15 minutes on the timeline, immediately on push when a status changes. Stale after 15 minutes without a push: hollow dots, "As of 9:26".
Deep linksRow → henosis://p/payments/s/checkout#approval-7f2; header → the approvals queue, the rail or the manager's usage view; the lock-screen ring → Usage.
ApproveThe button is an App Intent that sends the same vote as the session: a gate row sends 4 (the default pick); the Deny is a quiet circle. On a gate that needs a note, the tap opens the app on the sheet instead.
CountsNeeds you counts decisions I can still make (approvals plus handoffs offered to me); the app badge and the menu-bar number are the same count. Mentions never badge a widget.
WordsSame grammar as the toast and the inbox row: who · verb · what. Names in Instrument Serif, calls in mono, numbers in mono. "waits for you", "your 4 grants it", "nothing stops".
ScopeThe widget is configured per project (Payments) or for everything; the medium Tokens widget shows the project's budget, the small one shows mine.
CalmPause notifications shows the moon in the count's place; nothing pulses; the widget still updates. Reduced motion stops the running dots.
