Six kinds, one shape

Crease on the left names the kind; the serif names the who; one sentence says what; the second line says where and what it means for you. At most two buttons and an Open. Nothing is a tool here: agents join, finish and ask like people do.

  • News (mention, joined, done) drains over 8 s along the apricot life line; hover or focus pauses it.
  • Joined carries the mark closing its arcs; it is the only toast with motion of its own.

Stacking and dismissal

Bottom-right, above the composer, newest at the bottom so it rises in from the corner. Three show; older ones step up and dim. A fourth folds the oldest into the navy tail, which counts what waits in the inbox and opens it.

Fold
past three, the oldest news folds into the tail first; decisions fold last
Dismiss
