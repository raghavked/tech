# Agent profile · notes

## The idea
An agent gets the same kind of page a person would: a profile that answers "who is this member, who does it belong to, what has it done for us, and when did it join". The header reads like a roster row grown up (name in serif, Team pill, owner, crew, team, model line, then what it is on right now with the baton holder and who is in), four tiles put the week in numbers (sessions, tokens against the soft budget with a day sparkline and plan-vs-actual savings, approvals earned with the average rating, memory added with its one conflict), and the two columns below are the receipts: every session with its state and spend, every approval it earned with who granted it and the stars, the membership facts, the facts it remembered in Ana's name, and the manners it keeps. The joined-the-circle note sits on an apricot wash with the mark and says the product's point in one line: it joined the same morning as Ana.

## What to keep
- The header grammar: serif name + Team pill, then "Owned by Ana · Crew Invoice rollout · Payments team", the mono model line, then "Working on *italic* [stack] Ana has the baton · Bo and Dee are in". On mobile the same four lines stack under the avatar row via grid areas; nothing is dropped except the model line.
- Agent avatar is the rail's agent shape (rounded with one square corner) on the brand gradient, carrying the mark; the only gradient on the screen besides the rail's New session button. The green live dot on its corner is the status.
- Stat tiles with serif numbers and a sans qualifier ("418k of 600k", "23 · 9 this week"); the tokens tile is the only one with a meter, a seven-day sparkline where today has a halo and past days sit at 55%, and the "saved 37k" line that comes from the plan estimates.
- Sessions rows use the rail's status drawings (green pulse, amber halo, grey disc, and a green check for united sessions) with the crew in serif behind the crew ring; the spend sits in mono at the end with a time under it.
- Approvals it earned: a drawn check, the risk class as a small label (irreversible is coral), who granted it in serif, the rule that was met ("two contributors at 4+"), the quote if there was one, and the stars with the raw picks in mono (5 · 4).
- Memory rows: the fact in reading type, then scope · "in Ana's name" · commit · day; the conflict is amber with the triangle, never red.
- Manners as four checked lines: plan first, release gate held by Bo who consumes the code, remembers in Ana's name, soft budget set by Bo.
- Dark mode works through tokens alone; the mark keeps both arc colours, and the joined wash stays apricot-soft on the navy surface.

## Open questions
- Name versus owner: the product calls the agent "Ana's agent", so the name is the owner's. If agents get their own names (or change owner), the header needs a second word and the "same morning as Ana" line needs a rule for agents made later.
- Should Sessions and Approvals be tabs with a filter bar on the real page (the lists will be long after a quarter), or stay as two stacked cards with "All sessions" links as here?
- "Approvals earned" counts grants; should refusals and revisions show here too, so a lead sees the whole record rather than the flattering half?
- The "saved 37k" line assumes plan estimates are always above actuals; what does the tile say in a week the agent overspent its plans?
- Message on an agent profile: does it open the session composer in Agent mode, or a chat with the owner? The button exists in two places (topbar and header); one should go on the real page.
- Is the model line (harness and model) something the team wants on a member's profile, or does it belong only in the Details drawer?
