Henosis · design exploration · system · avatar-system

One circle, two shapes.

An Cy De The Payments team in Proration: Ana has the baton, Bo and Cy are here, Dee is away, two agents with them.

A person is a circle: initials on a quiet tint, or a photo. An agent is the rounded square with one tight corner, the brand gradient, and the mark's centre inside. Both take the same sizes, the same presence dot, the same driver ring and the same stack, so a person and their agent read as two members of one circle and never as a user and a tool.

01

Anatomy

Shape says what kind of member. Everything else, size, edge, dot, ring, is shared.
An

A person is a circle

Initials of the first two letters of the name, as the name is written: An, Bo, Cy, Dee → De. Below 40 they set in Instrument Sans 600 at 39% of the size; from 40 up a name is a name and sets in Instrument Serif at 46%.

shapecircle, radius 50%
tintone of four washes hashed from the member id: apricot, navy, chocolate, sage, each mixed into the surface so ink stays legible in both themes
photofills the circle; the tint is the loading ground; a photo of an away person goes grey
edge2px of the ground it sits on (canvas, surface or rail), so stacks cut cleanly

An agent is the rounded square

Corners at 34% of the size, the bottom-left at 12%: it is the shape that speaks in the stream. The fill is the one navy→chocolate gradient; inside is the mark's centre, the two arcs around one dot, at 64% of the size; the person's arc is cream here, because chocolate on the gradient disappears at 28px. No letter: an agent is not a person with a different initial.

shaperadius 34% 34% 34% 12%; the tight corner always bottom-left
fill--gradient-brand, the same in dark; the arcs keep chocolate and apricot in every theme
below 24the arcs drop; only the apricot centre at double size remains
named"Ana's agent" may carry A with the centre tucked top-right (open question)

03

States

A state is a dot, a ring, or a fade. Never a colour change of the avatar itself: the tint is identity.
An

Plain

No dot means no presence claim: a roster of who is a member, an author line in replay, a memory entry's attribution.

Here

Green dot, bottom-right, cut out with the ground. On an agent the same dot means running and pulses; amber is waits at the gate, red blocked, slate paused.

An

Driver

One person per session has the baton: a 2px deep-apricot ring at a 2px gap. At 40 and up the baton disc joins it bottom-left. An agent is never the driver; the ring on an agent shape is a don't, shown here only to prove the ring fits.

.driver · .baton (≥40)
De

Away

Still a member, not here: the tint drains to 15% saturation at 60% opacity, a photo goes grey, and the dot becomes a hollow ring. An agent is never "away"; a stopped agent is paused at full colour with a slate dot.

.away + .pr.away
Cy

Selected / focus

The team glow, 5px of soft apricot, the same ring the composer and buttons use. Also the keyboard focus ring in a members list.

.selected · :focus-visible

Joined

One apricot ripple, 0.9s, when a member enters: a person into a session, an agent connecting. Off in calm mode and reduced motion. Never looped.

.joined (one-shot)

Named agent (proposal)

Where two agents sit side by side with no caption (a stack, a crew row), the owner's initial in italic serif tells them apart, with the centre tucked top-right. Only at 32 and up.

No name yet: the person icon on surface-3. Loading: a shimmer disc. More than the stack holds: "+3" in mono on surface-2, never an avatar shape.

06

Don'ts

Six ways to break the grammar, each next to the fix.
×An✓An

A person in the square

The rounded square is the agent's. A person is always a circle, even in an agent-shaped row.

×A✓

An agent as a round "A"

A circle with a letter is a person called A. The agent's shape and the mark's centre are what make it an agent.

×An✓An

Chocolate as a tint

Chocolate is the hand, for actions. An avatar in it looks like a button. Tints are washes, never solids.

×An✓An

A green ring for online

Rings mean the baton. Presence is the dot. Two things, two places.

×✓

An agent with the ring

The driver is a person. An agent says what it is doing with its dot, never that it is in charge.

×DEE✓De

Three letters, shouted

Two letters as the name is written. "Dee" is De, "Bo" is Bo. Never a surname initial unless two members share a first name.
