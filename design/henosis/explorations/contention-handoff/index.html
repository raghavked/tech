Rules the two notices share

The stripe says what kind

  • Amber on a contention: something is held. Apricot on a handoff: something live is moving between people. Green for a moment when either closes, then it folds into a divider.
  • Both keep the same head: a serif sentence in the product's voice, a plain rule under it, elapsed time on the right.
  • Neither notice blocks the stream. The agent keeps working around a contention; the driver keeps driving through an offer.

Who can act is written, not hidden

  • The rule names the picker ("until Ana picks one") and says when you can too ("you own this session").
  • Buttons appear only on the seats that can press them; the others get the sentence and a way to speak (Team chat, a third steer).
  • Your own direction is ringed in apricot and carries Withdraw; withdrawing is a quiet resolve.

The brief is computed, not written

  • Situation, open items and "since you were here" come from the ledger: step, held scopes, tokens, branch, who joined, what landed.
  • The offer shows a three-column preview; accepting moves the full brief into Details under Intent.
  • Keyboard: 1/2 pick, W withdraw, Y/N accept or decline. Calm mode stops the nudge and the ripple.
