# Onboarding · join the circle · notes

## The idea
Onboarding is two halves: on the navy side the circle itself, the mark with its arcs nearly closed and the centre drawn as a dashed ghost, the people and agents already inside it (Ana, Bo, Cy and two agents, Bo with the baton), and three small marks that tell the steps as stages of the mark (your arc alone, the agents' arc swinging in, the ring with its centre); on the cream page the three steps the app already has (who you are, your teams, your first session), done steps folded to one line. Step three is one card that holds the three ways in (new, join a live one, open by id) with the project, who sits with you, the title ("Invoice PDF: tax lines and proration"), the plan-first and budget policy, and one gradient action, "Enter the circle", whose caption says what happens next in the product's own words: the stream will say *Dee joins the circle*. The centre settles when you arrive: the dashed ghost in the big mark becomes the apricot dot the moment the session opens, and the same arcs-close plays in the stream.

## What to keep
- The three stages of the mark as the step indicator (one arc, two arcs apart, the closed ring with the centre): it teaches the mark while it shows progress, and the current stage sits on apricot wash.
- The ghost centre: a dashed apricot circle where the dot will be, the dot itself at 28%, so the big mark is visibly unfinished until you arrive; the stage card says why.
- "Already in" under the mark: a stack of people and agents in one row, Bo with the driver ring, and the sentence "Ana, Bo, Cy and two agents are in. Bo has the baton."
- Done steps fold to one serif line with the identity in mono ("dee · as in users.json") and a Change link; the team row keeps its chosen state with the apricot ring and Chosen in chocolate, and its projects as dots (green when a session is live).
- The role line on apricot wash under the team pick: what a contributor can do, and that Ana, the lead, can raise you.
- The first-session card: tabs for the three ways in, "Who sits with you" so the new person sees they do not start alone, policy as two tick cards with one-line consequences, the gradient action as the one hero moment on the screen.
- The terminal foot (`henosis join billing-page`) and "Skip, open the demo", for people who live in a terminal or are not ready.
- Dark: the side keeps the deeper rail navy, the status greens lift, the mark disc goes to the darker disc, the apricot ring on the chosen team still reads.
- Mobile: the side becomes a short navy header with the ring and the three stage tiles, then the same steps in one column with the action full width.

## Open questions
- The app's FirstRun has no "Join a live one" tab; the rail already lists live sessions. Is the tab worth having here, or does the new person see the rail first?
- "Who sits with you" is derived (Bo's session on Billing page is live); when the project has no live session the field goes away, or says "You start alone; invite from the share sheet". Which?
- The token budget shows the team's default as a tick-to-override. Today the field is a number input with no default; does Payments carry a per-team default at all?
- The role line names Ana as lead; the identity step today cannot know the role before /api/me answers. Should the line appear only once the server has replied?
- The big mark's centre settling on arrival is motion the app plays in the stream; should this page also play it (the dot pops, the ring ripples) before navigating, or does the arrival belong to the stream alone?
