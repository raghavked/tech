01The mapping

Dark mode is not an inversion. The frame darkens two notches, the page becomes a lighter navy than the frame so the rail still reads as the frame, the hand swaps from chocolate to apricot, and the pulse stays apricot everywhere. Shape, not hue, keeps the hand and the pulse apart at night.

07What holds, what to watch, what to fix

Twenty-four tokens remap and nineteen of them are right. The five that are wrong share one cause: a value hard-coded for cream (a gradient end, a status hue, a bezel) that nobody told about the night. The proposal is seven lines in tokens.css and no markup.

holds Keep

  • The frame stays the frame. Rail two notches below the page in both; the rail is never the same colour as a card.
  • The hand swaps to apricot, the ink flips with it. 11.6 : 1 on every primary button; no second accent needed.
  • The pulse never moves. Team wash, stars, running bar, glow, active ring, crew rings: apricot in both, so "live" means the same thing day and night.
  • Cream ink, not white. --ink #F0EAE0 keeps Instrument Serif warm; --ink-3 stays metadata-only.
  • Shadows go black and heavier. Composer, toast and human bubbles still lift; the rail's gradient button still reads.

watch Watch

  • Hairlines at .12 are faint on --surface; row dividers on the manager page nearly vanish. Consider .14 for dark.
  • The mark on the page. --mark-disc #0F131B on the canvas is 1.3 : 1; the arcs carry it, as intended, but at 20px the disc is gone. Fine in the rail, check in the topbar.
  • Two apricots. At night the hand and the pulse are the same hue. Shape keeps them apart (filled pill vs dot / ring / wash). Never a text link in apricot next to an apricot pill.
  • Agent bars in charts. --rail as a series colour is darker than the track at night; the board uses --surface-3 with a hairline. Name it.
  • The composer glow is louder on navy. Acceptable; revisit if the Team look editor lets a lead set a brighter highlight.

breaks Fix

  • Status colours are not remapped. ok 2.4, danger 2.3, info 1.9 : 1 on a dark card. The rail already carries the right values; lift them to the page tokens.
  • --gradient-brand loses its navy end on the hero and the gate stripe. Dark variant: both ends one notch up.
  • Selected segment inverts. .seg .on is --surface on --surface-2: lighter by day, darker by night. Add --seg-on.
  • Phone bezel is the dark canvas. .phone border is literally #1C2230. Add --bezel, #0F131B in dark.
  • Orbit loader's navy dot is --rail, invisible on the canvas at night. Use --surface-3 or --ink-3.
