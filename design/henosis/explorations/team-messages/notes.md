# Team messages · notes

## The idea
A team message is the people's voice inside the agent's room: it sits in the same stream as the goal and the agent's work, but on an apricot wash with a warm left edge, "to the team" in italic serif and a small "people" tag, so nobody mistakes it for something the agent will act on. Mentions are warm pills (solid apricot when it is you), a reply carries a one-line quote of what it answers, and a "New since you looked away" rule plus "Dee joins the circle" make the stream readable for someone coming back. The Team panel shows the same conversation at a second density (compact lines, the same mentions and quotes, the same unread rule) and says so, while the #billing group on mobile is where agents are members: an agent mention is a navy pill, and the agent's reply comes back in the chat with "agent replied · in reply to Dee · turn 8 · in session".

## What to keep
- The team bubble: apricot-soft wash, 3px apricot-deep left edge, no shadow; meta row of avatar, serif name, italic "to the team", the "people" tag, time. Your own messages a shade warmer.
- Mentions as pills: `@Bo` on apricot-soft with chocolate text, `@Ana` solid apricot when it is you, `@Billing page` navy with the agent's bubble-corner radius in group chats. Dark mode keeps ink on a quiet apricot instead of apricot-on-apricot.
- Reply as a quote block inside the bubble (serif name, one truncated line, links back) and "replying to Ana" in the meta, rather than threads.
- The composer in Team mode: apricot border and glow, "to the team" line that lists who is here and away, Team segment in solid apricot, and the mention completion docked inside the composer as a chip strip, with the agent shown dashed and greyed: "doesn't hear the team". Nothing floats over the stream.
- The hover toolbar on a team message: Reply, Link, and "Tell the agent", which quotes the message into the Agent composer as a steer. That is the only bridge between the two conversations and it is explicit.
- The hint under the composer: "Team messages stay with the people here and in the replay. Billing page never hears them."
- The Team panel chat as the same notes at a second density, with the note that says it is the same conversation; "You" with a warm bubble, others as plain lines.
- In a group chat an agent reply is plain prose with a 2px navy→chocolate left rule and a live dot on its avatar; the member list in the mention popup gives agents a role line "Ana's agent · running" and a footer "Mentioning an agent steers its session".

## Open questions
- "Tell the agent" turns a team message into a steer; should the steer show the quoted team message to the agent verbatim, or only what the person types after it?
- Should the Team panel chat collapse to only the unread tail when the panel is open beside the stream, since both show the same notes?
- The group mention popup lists agents under people; when a crew has four agents, do we group them under the crew name or show the crew itself as a mentionable member?
- Mobile group chat: the agent reply meta wraps to two lines; which of "agent replied", "in reply to Dee", "turn 8" and "in session" can go on small screens?
- Reactions on team messages are not designed; a 👍 on Bo's answer might be enough to avoid "ok" messages in the stream.
