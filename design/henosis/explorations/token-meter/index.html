02

The chip

One mono number in the title row. It says what the branch has spent; the hairline under it says how much of the budget that is. Colour arrives only when a budget turns, and never anywhere else in the row.

EmptyBefore the first turn. Faint; no hairline until a budget is set..meter.none
No budgetJust the number. The rail and the project page show the same figure..meter
Live, underThe apricot hairline weaves while the agent spends; the ring turns with it. The tick marks 80%..meter.live
NearingPast 80%: number and bar go amber. Nothing else changes..meter.warn
OverPast 100%: red, bar full, the percent keeps counting. It is a number people watch, not a gate..meter.over

Counting

The chip counts input plus output. Cache reads and writes show in the breakdown but never move the bar: the policy budgets what the agent asks for, not what the cache gives back.

Rounding

Under 10k: one decimal (9.9k). 10k and up: one decimal to 99.9k, then whole (120k). The percent is whole. The popover and the drawer show full numbers with thousands separators.

Reach

Hover or focus opens the breakdown; click pins it; Escape closes. Accessible name: "Tokens: 12,400 used, 62% of the budget". Calm mode stops the weave and the ring.

04

The budget bar

One hairline, 3px in the title row, 5px in cards and the drawer. A tick at 80% so the amber moment is visible before it happens. Colour is the only thing that changes between states.

Fresh0% · no turns yetEmpty track with the tick. Says a budget exists.
Under, idle31% · 6.2k of 20kApricot gradient, still.
Under, live62% · 12.4k of 20kThe gradient weaves while a turn runs; stops at turn end.
Nearing84% · 16.8k of 20kAmber from 80%. No motion: a warning does not pulse.
Over112% · 22.4k of 20kRed, full, with a faint red halo. The number carries the overflow.
Done, underfinished at 58%In the handoff brief and the replay, a finished branch under budget turns green once.
Where it appears. Under the chip in the title row; under the totals of a plan card; at the top of Usage in the drawer; as a 2px hair at the end of each rail card when a budget is set; in the manager's tokens-by-agent table.
What moves it. Only input plus output. Cache figures are shown beside it, never inside it, so a session that reads a lot from cache does not look expensive.
The 80% tick. A 1px line in ink-3 at 80% of the track. It is the only annotation on the bar and it is always there when a budget is, so amber never surprises.
Over 100%. The fill stops at the end; the chip's percent and the drawer's "over by 2.4k" carry the overflow. We do not draw past the track.
Soft. Nothing stops at the budget. The drawer says so under the bar, in one line, and the plan card repeats it in its foot.
Motion. The weave is the only motion in the meter and it means "spending now". Reduced motion and calm mode freeze it to the still gradient.
