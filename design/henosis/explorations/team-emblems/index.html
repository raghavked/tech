Henosis · brand exploration team-emblems v1 · 06 Oct 2026

Twelve teams, one ring.

Every team in Henosis wears the same mark: a person's arc and an agent's arc that close around one centre. A lead may dress it in the Team look editor with three things only: the accent for the person's arc, the highlight for the agent's arc and the centre, and a one- or two-letter emblem that takes the place of the centre dot. The disc, the stroke and the join never change, so twelve teams still read as one company.

01

What a team may change

Three dials, in this order. Everything else is the mark.

A
Accent — the person's arc
Also the team's primary button, links and the driver halo. One of the twelve curated accents; a lead cannot type a hex.
--team-accent
B
Highlight — the agent's arc and the centre
Also the Team pill, live glows and team messages. Must sit at 3 : 1 or better against the navy disc.
--team-highlight
C
Emblem — one or two letters in the centre
Instrument Serif, title case, set in the highlight on navy and in the accent on cream. Replaces the centre dot; never sits beside it. Below 24 px the dot returns.
--team-emblem
·
Fixed — disc, stroke, geometry, join
Navy disc, 10 / 96 stroke, arcs that meet at 12 and 6 o'clock, and the joining motion: arcs close, then the centre appears. No team may move these.

05

Not a team ring

What the editor refuses, so a lead never has to be told. Each of these turns a crest back into a badge.

Three letters

Two is the most the ring can hold at 32 px.

Emblem and dot

The emblem is the centre. Never both.

One colour for both arcs

A person's arc and an agent's arc must stay two colours.

A dim highlight

Slate on navy is 1.6 : 1. The pulse must glow.

A status colour

Green, amber and red belong to running, waiting and blocked.

Another disc

The disc is navy, or absent on navy. Not the accent, not white.

Lowercase or a sans

Title case in Instrument Serif, like every name in the product.

A logo in the ring

The emblem is letters. A team's icon goes on its project page, not in the mark.
