The rules

01
Three clocks
120 ms for hover and focus, 200 ms for press and toggles, 320 ms for anything that arrives or settles. Nothing in a micro-interaction is slower than 320.
120200320
Two curves
Plain ease for colour, shadow and opacity. The mark's curve (.3,.9,.3,1) for anything that moves: a thumb, a handle, a card lifting.
Colour is state
Hover uses surface-2 and shadow. Focus uses the team glow. Selection and the live thing use apricot. The hand (chocolate) only on the primary action and the pressed check.
Calm keeps the state
With Calm on or reduced motion, every end state stays: the glow, the apricot row, the slid thumb, the drawn check. Only the change is instant. Touch has no hover: press is the hover.
