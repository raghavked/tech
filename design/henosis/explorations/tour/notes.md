# Tour · five coach marks in the register · notes

## The idea
The tour is five coach marks on the real session, not a slideshow: one dim, one lit target with the apricot halo, one mark at a time, and the stream scrolls each target into view before the mark rises (the rail's agent cards, the attributed stream, the plan card, the release-gate notice, the Agent/Team composer). The ring in the corner of every mark closes one segment per step, chocolate for the step you are on and apricot for the ones done, with the centre drawn as a dashed ghost until the last button, "Close the ring", pops the dot and the toast says *Dee is in the circle*. Dee is the new contributor in every piece of content: the rail's "me" row, the topbar stack, the divider *Dee joins the circle* and the composer hint, so the tour teaches the manners (plan first, ratings, the gate held by the consumers, Team never reaching the agent) on the very session she will steer.

## What to keep
- The mark anchored to real elements, lit with the apricot halo, and the stream scrolled so the target is first in view; nothing is moved or mocked, only dimmed.
- The five in this order: rail, stream, plan, gate, composer. It walks from "who is here" to "what you do", and ends on the control the new person will touch first.
- The ring-progress: five segments, chocolate = now, apricot = done, ghost centre until the end. It is the mark's own story as a step counter.
- The "Your rating counts once" tip on apricot wash in step 3 and the "Approve stays live under the dim" rule in step 4: the tour never gets between a person and a vote.
- Copy in the product's voice: "Plans come first.", "The gate is held by the people who consume the code.", "Agent, or Team."
- Mobile: the mark is a bottom sheet with a grab handle, the target above it, Skip in the corner; the topbar drops the status chip so the title stays whole.
- Dark: the dim goes near-black at 62 %, the lit card keeps its surface, the halo keeps the apricot, the primary button turns apricot on navy as the tokens say.
- Keyboard: → / ← step, Esc skips, focus trapped in the mark, the dim is aria-hidden.

## Open questions
- Step 2 lights three messages (goal, reply, team note) in one halo. If the goal is long or the reply has an open shell block, the halo could exceed the viewport. Light the goal only, or light the three but cap the halo to the viewport and scroll inside it?
- The tour wants a session with a plan and a release gate to point at. When Dee's first session has neither (a fresh solo one), do steps 3 and 4 fall back to a drawn example card, or does the tour wait for the first real plan and resume then?
- The last button carries the one gradient on the screen, but the rail's "New session" is also gradient-brand and sits on the same screen. Dim the rail's gradient under the layer, or make "Close the ring" chocolate like the other Next buttons?
- Should an arriving approval for Dee pause the tour (as written) or end it? Pausing keeps the ring's progress; ending is simpler and the tour can be reopened from Settings.
- Does the tour belong to the first *session* (as here) or to the first *project page*? A manager's first run would see a different five.
