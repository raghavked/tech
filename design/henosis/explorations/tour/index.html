Rules

When it shows

  • Once, on the first session a member opens after joins the circle; never on the demo session, never for an agent.
  • Starts 600 ms after the stream has folded, so the targets exist. If the rail is folded (mobile), step 1 opens it first.
  • Reopen from Settings · Help · Take the tour or the ? sheet. Replays from step 1.

What it never does

  • Never covers an approval that is yours: step 4's notice stays live under the dim, and a new approval for you pauses the tour with the notice lit.
  • Never moves the composer or the rail; the dim is a layer, the targets are the real elements, lit with the apricot halo.
  • Never auto-advances. Esc skips, → / ← step, focus stays in the mark; the dim is aria-hidden.

Motion and themes

  • Each mark rises (rise .32s) after the target scrolls into view; the ring segment fills with a draw. Calm mode and reduced motion: cut, no scroll animation.
  • Dark: the dim is near-black at 62 %, the halo keeps the apricot, the lit card keeps its surface, the primary button is apricot on navy as the tokens say.
  • Mobile: the mark is a sheet from the bottom; the target sits above it; a swipe down is Skip.
