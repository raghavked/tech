Rules

What never happens.

The lines that keep six states from becoming a spinner.

The ring never reopens to say “waiting”. Joining is the only swing. Thinking breathes one half; blocked opens the top seam by retracting, never by swinging apart.

Nothing travels around the ring. The retired bead stays retired. The baton is the centre crossing a diameter, once, in 500 ms.

Status colour never touches the mark. Blocked is a shape; the amber lives in the word beside it. At 16 px the three favicons differ in shape.

One breathing mark per surface. The rail breathes on running cards only; the topbar carries one; the tab carries one.

The centre pops once, at a join. Resting marks are the end frame. Under Calm the once-states are their end frame and the loops are the closed ring.

Below 24 px there is no swing. The closed mark fades in over 200 ms; waiting is the halo; blocked is the open seam.

The lean is the baton, not an error. 5 units toward the holder: 1.7 px at 32, 14 px at 270. It is never on the brand row, the tab or paper; those marks have no driver.

Only transform, translate, opacity, stroke-width and stroke-dasharray move. No filters, no layout, no colour on the arcs.
