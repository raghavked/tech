Rules

What keeps it ours.

Motion means joining or living. The arcs swing only on a join. Thinking never reopens the ring: the ring is whole, half of it is awake. Blocked opens the seam but does not move it. The baton is the only thing that travels, once, half a turn, under the seam.

The hand is a verb, the pulse is alive. Everything that breathes, glows or ripples is apricot, on both arcs. Chocolate never pulses. Status colours never touch the mark; the word beside it carries them.

The seam is on the side that must act. Blocked retracts the person's arc because the missing thing is a hand. The agent's arc never retracts in the product (a server gone is the ghost centre, not a seam).

Rest is the end state. Six states land on mark.svg. Calm and reduce show the end frame: closed ring, open seam, hairline over the holder. Nothing is hidden until it plays.

One moving mark per screen. If a loader is joining, a join line shows the closed ring and only the avatar ripples. Thinking at 20 px in the rail is allowed beside a 40 px settled in the stream: breath is not a swing.

Not a spinner, not a sparkle. Nothing orbits, nothing is purple, nothing is a badge. At 16 px the state is weight and seam, not colour: the three favicons differ in shape.
