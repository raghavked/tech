The rating control

One to five stars with a word under the cursor: Not yet, Risky, Fine, Good, Ship it. It is the same control on a plan and on a release gate, in the stream, the queue, the inbox and the palette. A pick is a vote; the kernel decides.

01

The scale

Each star has a word, and the word says what the pick does. Three approves a plan on its own; four is where a release gate starts counting. The hairline after the third star is that threshold, shown only under a gate.

No notes. Used sparingly it means something; the average shows it.

  • 1
    Label. "Your rating" on a gate, "Rate the plan" on a plan. Never "Rating:" with a colon.
  • 2
    Five stars. A radiogroup of five radios. The pick and everything before it fill in apricot-deep; a preview fills in apricot, lighter, so you can see the pick under the preview.
  • 3
    The gate tick. A 1.5px hairline between 3 and 4, only when the approval is under a ratings rule. It says where the count starts without a sentence.
  • 4
    The word. Instrument Serif italic, coloured by level: danger for 1, warn for 2, ink for 3, ok for 4 and 5. It is also the accessible name of the star ("4 of 5, Good") and is read by the live region.
  • 5
    Note. One line, grows to four. Optional at 3 and up; nudged at 1 and 2. ⌘↩ sends the vote with the note.
  • 6
    Action. The button carries the number it will send: "Approve with 4". With no pick it still reads "Approve with 4" and the hint says why. On a plan the button is "Rate".

06

Keyboard, pointer and semantics

The control is one tab stop. Inside it the arrow keys move a preview; nothing is sent until you confirm. The same five keys work on the row under the cursor in the queue and in the palette, so a rating never needs the mouse.

KeyInside the control
TabEnters the group on the pick, or on 4 (the default) when nothing is picked. The word shows.
←→ ↓↑Move the preview one star; stops at 1 and 5, does not wrap. The word follows.
1 … 5Jump the preview to that star.
Space ↩Pick the previewed star. The star pops, the button label updates, focus moves to the note.
0 ⌫Clear the pick; the word goes back to the ghost "Pick one".
EscDrop the preview and show the pick again. Does not leave the group.
⌘↩From the note: send the vote with the pick, or with 4 when nothing is picked.
pointerHover previews, click picks, clicking the pick again clears it. Leaving the control drops the preview.
Accessibility tree
// one group, five radios, one live word
group "Your rating" role=radiogroup
  radio "1 of 5, Not yet"  checked=false
  radio "2 of 5, Risky"    checked=false
  radio "3 of 5, Fine"     checked=false
  radio "4 of 5, Good"     checked=true  ← tabindex 0
  radio "5 of 5, Ship it"  checked=false
status aria-live=polite  "Good, 4 of 5"
textbox "A note with your vote"  multiline
button "Approve with 4"
// under a release gate, the group also describes the bar:
// aria-describedby → "needs 2 at 4+"
Roving tabindex, not five tab stops. Contrast: the filled star is a shape, not text, so apricot-deep on cream is allowed; the word carries the meaning in ink-level colours (4.6:1 or better in both themes). Calm mode removes the pop and the drawn check.
Pick · popon click, Space or EnterThe picked star scales .6 → 1.12 → 1 in 360ms; the stars before it fill without motion. The word crossfades 120ms.
Grant · drawwhen the kernel grantsThe check draws in 500ms, then "You approved · 4 of 5" rises in. The gate segment lights with it.
Gate opens · ripplewhen the second 4+ landsThe notice's gradient edge ripples once in apricot and the step row goes to running. Calm mode: none of the above.
