Type scale and rhythm

Three voices and fourteen steps. The serif says who; the sans says what; the mono says how much. Every line height sits on a 4px beat, and prose in the column never runs past 600px.

The beat

Line heights are 16, 20, 24, 32 or 44: every step lands on the 4px grid drawn behind the samples. Vertical space between elements uses the same grid, so a caption under a name under a title still adds up.

Weights

Serif400 only, with italic. The italic is the emphasis: joins, · crew, a team message's to. Sans400 reads, 500 is a control, 600 is a label. Never bold inside a paragraph beyond a single phrase. Mono400 lines, 500 for a figure or a total. Always tabular-nums.

Fallbacks

Before the webfonts land, Iowan Old Style or Georgia stands in for the serif, the system sans for Instrument Sans, Menlo for the mono. The steps keep their px sizes; nothing reflows when the real faces arrive because line heights are fixed.

Tracking

  • Serif at 26 and above: -0.01em. Figures: -0.02em.
  • Kicker: +0.08em, uppercase, the only all-caps on a page.
  • Nothing else is tracked. Mono is never tracked.

03The column

The stream is 780px wide with 24px gutters: 732px of content. Cards and tables use all of it. Prose stops at 600px, about 85 characters of Body, which is where a line of Instrument Sans is still easy to return from. Ana's own messages already stop short; this makes the agent's do the same.

04Numbers

06Rules of thumb

The three voices only work if each stays in its lane. These are the mistakes a new screen is most likely to make.

Serif names, never explains

YesUnited pdf-layout into main
NoThe plan waits for two ratings before any tool call above read is allowed.

A serif line is a name or a short announcement: one line, no wrapping. If it wraps, it is reading, and it goes to sans.

Mono counts, never speaks

Yestest billing/proration 14 passed · 0.8k
NoI ran the tests and all fourteen of them passed.

Mono is for what a machine said or counted: a path, a verb, a figure. A number inside a sentence stays sans: "two tests cover the leap-day case".

One beat, one measure

Yes15.5/24, 600px, 12px between paragraphs
No15px/1.6 stretched across all 732px

Line heights in px, not ratios, so two steps next to each other share the grid. Prose never runs wider than the measure, even when its card does.
