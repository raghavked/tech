Henosis · design exploration · icon set · system

Forty icons, one stroke:
the line a hand and an agent share.

A line icon set on a 16 grid in the Henosis stroke: 1.5 units, round caps, round joins, nothing filled. The same geometry the app's Icon component already draws, extended to the nouns the brief names and the app lacks glyphs for: agent, crew, group, baton, unite, plan, rating, tokens, gate. Every dot in the set is the mark's centre, drawn as a stroked circle, so an icon is always one path and one colour.

01

The stroke

One grid, one weight, four keyshapes. A new icon is right when it sits inside the safe area, touches a keyshape, and has no line thinner or thicker than 1.5.

16 grid, 1.5 safe area

Paths live inside 1.5 to 14.5 so the stroke's half-width never leaves the box. Ink may touch 0.75, never 0.

viewBox 0 0 16 16 · fill none · stroke currentColor

Stroke 1.5, caps and joins round

One weight at every size. 24 px renders 2.25, 12 px renders 1.125; the set is never redrawn with a heavier cut, unlike the mark.

stroke-width 1.5 · linecap round · linejoin round

Dots are stroked circles

A dot is a circle of radius .75 drawn in the stroke, which reads as a solid 3 px bead at 16 px. The join icon's centre is radius 1. No fills, ever, so every icon stays one path and one colour.

M8 8m-.75 0a.75.75 0 1 0 1.5 0a.75.75 0 1 0-1.5 0

Four keyshapes

Circle r 6; square 11 at 2.5; portrait 9×12 at 3.5/2; landscape 12×9 at 2/3.5. Round things sit on the circle and look as big as square things that sit on the square.

circle r6 · square 11 · portrait 9×12 · landscape 12×9

Radius 1 on corners, 1.5 on heads

Rectangles round at 1; an agent's head rounds at 1.5 so it is read as a head, not a card. Gaps between strokes are at least 1 unit of clear air (2.5 centre to centre).

rx 1 · agent head rx 1.5 · min gap 1

Nouns from the mark

Agent, crew, group, join and handoff all carry the centre dot; a person never does. The ring of the join icon is the mark's two arcs with seams at 12 and 6 o'clock, never a closed circle and never the old ring-and-bead.

person: no dot · agent: dot · join: two arcs + centre

Sizes and colour

Six sizes, one drawing. An icon takes the colour of its text and never carries its own; the only colours an icon may be are the ones a word may be in the same place.

Size ladder

Below 12 the set is not used: a 10 px glyph is a dot or a word. Above 32 an icon is a picture, not an icon; empty states use the mark or an illustration instead.

Where an icon may be which colour

ink-2 default beside text: steps, meta, drawer, inbox rows
--ink-2
ink hover, the current item, tile previews
--ink
accent-fg on accent inside a primary button, with its word
--accent-fg
accent the title of a plan card or gate notice, the active mobile tab
--accent

--rail-muted
apricot on the rail only the active nav item: the pulse marks what is live
--apricot

05

Don'ts

Six ways an icon stops being a Henosis icon.

Don't change the weight

2 looks bold, 1 looks thin beside Instrument Sans. 1.5 at every size.

Don't fill

A filled glyph is a different family and cannot take a status colour cleanly. Dots are strokes.

Don't square the caps

Round caps and joins are the stroke. Square ends read as a different, colder set.

Don't draw the old mark

The old mark. Join is two arcs with seams and a centre, never a ring with a bead on it.

Don't colour a noun

Tokens, crew and project take the text's colour. Only approve, contention and reject may wear ok, warn and danger.

Don't crowd the box

Nothing past the safe area, and no two strokes closer than one unit. If a detail needs it, the detail goes.
