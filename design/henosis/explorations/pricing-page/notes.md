# Pricing page

## The idea
Two meters and nothing else: an active session-hour ($0.90, billed by the minute only while an agent is running a step or a person is steering) and an approver seat ($24 a month, only for the people who rate plans and hold release gates). Everything the brief calls the selling moment is free by design: observers, contributors and agents join for nothing, so "Dee joins the circle with her agent" is the one highlighted row of the estimator and it adds no line until she first holds a gate, prorated to the day. The page proves the rule with a real Tuesday on the Billing page session (9 h 40 m wall clock, 5 h 05 m billed) and with the September invoice for the Payments team, written out with included hours, Dee's prorated seat, VAT and the token usage that is deliberately not on it.

## What to keep
- "Pay for the hours the ring is closed" as the framing: waiting on people is never billed, which is also an argument for plan-first and gates rather than against them.
- The estimator in the navy stage with the join row in the rail-active apricot wash; the apricot CTA on navy (the brand gradient on navy lost its left edge, so the primary inside the stage is the pulse colour).
- The session strip: apricot = billed, apricot wash = waiting, grey = paused, with the people's events underneath. It teaches the meter in one glance.
- The invoice with proration and the included-hours line as explicit lines, not a buried rate, and the footer saying model tokens are the provider's.
- Seats defined as "a hand on the gate", tied to the ratings rule in the spec, not to a job title.

## Open questions
- Is $0.90/h and $24/seat the right shape, or should Team be a flat base plus hours? The free 20 hours on Team keeps Circle and Team one line apart; a base fee would make that a cliff.
- Do forks and crews metering their own hours feel fair, or does it punish exploration? An alternative is to bill only the branch that unites.
- A manager who rates a plan once takes a seat for the rest of the month (prorated start, prorated stop). Is "the day she first holds a gate" discoverable enough, or does Checkout need a confirm step the first time?
- The Org tier says "Volume" with no number; sales will want an anchor (e.g. 2,000 committed hours at $0.70).
- Hard monthly cap: the FAQ promises it, the app has only soft token budgets today. Needs a product decision before this ships.
