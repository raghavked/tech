Active means someone is doing something

The agent is running a step, or a person is steering. Measured by the minute from the session log, so the number on your invoice is the same number the replay shows.

Waiting is never billed

A plan waiting for ratings, a release gate waiting for a quorum, a paused agent, a session idle overnight. We would rather you take the time to look.

A seat is a hand on the gate

Only people who rate plans or grant approvals under a ratings rule take a seat. The first time Dee rates a plan, her seat starts that day. Remove her from the gate and it stops.

Joining is the whole point, so it is free

A new engineer and a new agent enter the same circle on the same day. Neither adds a line to the invoice. Agents are members, not metered tools.

Questions

Asked before the first invoice

What if two people steer the same hour?

One session-hour. Hours belong to the session, not the people in it. Four people in one session cost the same as one.

Do forks and crews multiply the bill?

A fork has its own agent runner, so its active minutes count. Uniting it back is free. A crew is two sessions; each meters itself.

Is a manager a seat?

Only if they rate plans or grant gated approvals. Reading the manager overview, the inbox and the replay never takes a seat.

What about the tokens?

Your model provider bills them. Henosis shows what every turn cost, estimates them in every plan, and reports the savings. We do not mark them up.

Can we set a ceiling?

Yes. A hard monthly cap on hours pauses new work at the limit and tells the people in the session why; soft token budgets per session stay as they are.

How does Dee's seat start and stop?

It starts the day she first holds a gate and is prorated to the day. Take her off the gate and the seat ends that day; the invoice writes both dates out.
