Rules

  • The chip always says how the next send lands; the popover only edits it.
  • Team tint uses the pulse (apricot) at 14%, never the hand.
  • Interrupt is the only warn colour in the composer; nothing else is amber.
  • Offline: the field never locks; the send becomes a clock and the hint says Reconnecting.
  • Calm mode: no caret blink, no dots, no ring.

What changes on a phone

The Agent/Team toggle loses its icons; the + button folds into the chip (tap the chip to open); the popover becomes a bottom sheet with a Done button that repeats the chip's words; the hint line is dropped because Enter is a soft key. The composing line stays: it is how two people on phones avoid steering the same scope at once.

What stays

The chip, the apricot team wash, the amber interrupt, the clock send when offline, and the plan-waits line above the field. The send is still 36px and still presses.
