Anatomy

01

Three lines, every one earning its place: the head says who and whether it needs you, the middle says what it is on, the foot says with whom and at what cost.

  1. 1Status dotThe five status words as one 8px dot: green pulses while running, amber for an approval, coral when blocked, grey disc when paused, a ring when idle, a faint ring when closed.
  2. 2Pending badgeApprovals waiting, in mono on amber. Pops when the count changes. Absent at zero; the card never shows a 0.
  3. 3Doing lineAn italic serif lead and a plain tail, two lines at most: working on the goal, waiting on an approval, last the summary.
  4. 4Solo / Team pillTeam when more than one person is in the session or it is in a crew; apricot so it reads from across the room. Solo is an outline and keeps its place so the foot never jumps.
  5. 5WhoUp to three online people, the driver ringed in apricot, then "with Ana, Bo"; a solo card names its owner instead.
  6. 6TokensCompact spend at the end of the line, with a 2px hairline when the policy sets a budget: apricot, amber past 80%, coral past 100%. It is watched, never a gate.

The six states

02

The status word, the dot and the lead word always agree. Only the head and the lead change between states; the shape of the card does not, so the rail stays still while agents move through their day.

In the mobile rail

06

The rail becomes a sheet. The same card, two points larger, with a 44px tap target per line of the foot.

Agents 4

The sheet opens from the bottom tab. Awaiting-approval cards sort first, then running, then the rest by last activity, so the one that needs a thumb is under it.

No hover exists here: the peek row's actions live in a long-press menu with the same three verbs, Pause, Pin, More.

The budget hairline is kept; it is 2px and reads at arm's length because it is the only colour in the foot besides the pill.
