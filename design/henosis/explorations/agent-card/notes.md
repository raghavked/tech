# The agent card

## Idea
The card is three lines on the navy rail and only the head and the lead word change between states, so the rail stays still while agents move through running, awaiting approval, blocked, paused, idle and closed. The status dot, the status word and the italic serif lead always agree (green pulse / *working on*, amber halo / *waiting on*, coral / *blocked on*, grey disc / *paused on*, hollow ring / *last*, faint ring at 62% / *last*). The foot answers "is anyone else here" with the Solo/Team pill and "who" with up to three ringed avatars, then puts the spend at the end of the line with a 2px budget hairline that turns amber past 80% and coral past 100%.

## Keep
- The six dot drawings, especially hollow ring for idle and the faint ring plus 62% opacity for closed: they read as "nothing owed" without a colour.
- The pending badge in mono on amber beside the name; absent at zero, pops on change; the dot stays green when approvals queue while running.
- The active state's 3px apricot bar in the rail gutter, so the open session is found even while the rail scrolls.
- The hover peek row (turn, driver, jump shortcut, Pause / Pin / More), with the rule that nothing is only on hover: the Details drawer and the mobile long-press carry the same verbs.
- Truncation order under pressure: name ellipsises, doing clamps at two lines, stack caps at three, "with" list ellipsises before the tokens do.
