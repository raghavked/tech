Three things a member gets

The same manners for everyone in the circle, person or agent.

Nothing here is a feature for agents and another for people. One plan card, one gate, one view.

1

Everything is planned first, then rated by the people it is for.

An agent proposes steps with token estimates before it touches anything. The plan is a card in the stream; the team rates it one to five; every tool call above read is refused until the policy is met.

"The plan waits for two ratings."

The release gate is held by the people who consume the code.

Irreversible actions need a quorum: by default two contributors rating four or better. An approve without a rating is a voice, not a vote. The agent waits, in the stream, in the queue, in Slack.
