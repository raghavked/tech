# Members and agents roster · notes

## The idea
One list for the whole circle: people and agents are rows of the same table, with the same columns (member, role, team, what they are on, joined), and the list is sorted by the day each one joined, so a person and their agent sit together as the pair they are ("12 Mar 2026 · same day as Ana"). The lede is one sentence a lead can read in three seconds ("4 people and 4 agents are members of Payments. Dee joined the circle yesterday, with her agent."), the chips filter the one list rather than splitting it into two, and a green ring says who is online while the status dot says what an agent is doing. Selecting an agent opens its profile card on the right: what it is on, who it is with, the morning it joined (with the mark), the week's tokens against budget, plans rated, approvals granted, facts remembered, its groups, and its manners (plan first, gate held by Bo who consumes the code, remembers in Ana's name).

## What to keep
- One table, one row grammar for people and agents: round avatar for a person, the agent avatar shape for an agent; the sub-line carries "Online · driving Proration" or "Ana's agent · with Bo, Dee".
- Role column as pills: Owner (filled), Lead (apricot outline), Member (outline); agents get "Agent" with their Team/Solo pill inside it, so the Team pill means the same thing it means in the rail.
- Team column: the crew in serif with the crew ring, the team name under it; "no crew yet" for the newest pair.
- On column in the voice of the rail: the thing named in italic serif, the status dot for agents (running, waits at the gate, blocked, paused).
- Joined column in mono with a second line that reads the date for you: "made the team", "7 months", "yesterday", and "same day as Ana" in apricot for every agent; that line is the product's point.
- "New" pill and a rippling avatar for whoever joined yesterday; the lede names them.
- The agent profile card: identity, status line, two actions and a more menu, then On / Joined / This week / Groups / Manners, with the joining note on an apricot wash carrying the mark.
- Mobile: rail and card go, rows become two-line cards with the same five facts, the Invite button becomes an icon.

## Open questions
- Sorting by joined date is what makes the pairs visible; should the default be joined, or name, with the pair shown another way (an indent or a bracket)?
- Selecting a person: is there a person profile card too (role, their agents, groups, tokens), or does a person row go to their page?
- The "same day as Ana" line assumes an agent joins the day its owner does; what should it say for an agent made months later, or one handed to another owner?
- Roles today are member / lead / admin in the fleet events; the card says Owner for Bo because the rail does. Which word does the product use?
- Manners on the card are copy today (plan first, gate holder, memory attribution); should they be live facts read from the project's policy, and editable by a lead from here?
- Does "Live 3" filter on agents only, or include online people (the green rings)?
