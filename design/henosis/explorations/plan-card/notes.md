# Plan card · notes

## The idea
The plan card is a ledger that lives: the same card is proposed, rated, run and closed without changing shape, so a person who learned it once reads every later moment at a glance. Each step is a row with its risk word, the tokens it actually took beside the estimate, and a state in the number ring (hairline pending, apricot pulse running, green check done); the totals line keeps the actual fill against the budget with the estimate as a tick, so estimate-versus-actual is one bar, not two. The rating row is the team's act: your five stars and a note, the count and average against the policy in mono, the raters as chips with their stars and words, and under it the owner's three decisions, with one sentence saying what each does to the agent.

## What to keep
- One card, four moments: Waiting for ratings (amber pulse in the status), Approved/running (green stripe), Done (ledger with "2,380 under · 14% under estimate"), Revision asked (dimmed, grey stripe, "Replaced by a newer plan" with a jump link). The stripe colour on the left edge carries the status before the words do.
- Step row grid: ring · title + detail · risk pill · actual · estimate. The actual is ink, "so far" in chocolate while running, amber (never red) when a step runs over; the estimate stays faint mono with a tilde.
- Risk words as pills: read (neutral), write (apricot), exec (navy wash), external (amber), irreversible (red). The irreversible step says "waits at the release gate" in its detail, so the plan and the gate point at each other.
- The totals meter: actual fill, estimate tick with "est", caption "14.1k of 40k". Budget stays soft: the bar turns, nothing cuts off.
- Rating row and progress copy straight from the app: "1 of 2 ratings · 4.0 average · waiting · needs 2 at 3+", "approved by the team's ratings" in green, "approved by Ana" in serif when an owner overrides.
- Raters as chips with a serif name, mini stars and the note in quotes; people who have not rated yet as dashed chips, so the owner sees who the plan is waiting on.
- Decisions: Approve now (primary) only while proposed; Ask to revise and Reject stay while approved; one line under them explains the consequence. Reject is a ghost button in danger ink, never a filled red.
- Mobile: risk and estimate fold under the detail as a small meta line; the actual stays in the right column; stars and progress share one row with the note below.

## Open questions
- Should "Approve now" carry the owner's own rating too (approve with 4), so the ledger of ratings stays complete, or is the override deliberately a separate voice?
- When a step runs over its estimate, should the card say why (the agent's own one-line reason, as in step 3 of the Done state), and who writes it?
- The running step shows "3,640 so far": does it update live per tool call, or per turn, and does the budget meter animate with it?
- The superseded plan keeps its full step list; should it collapse to the head and the decision line, with the steps behind a disclosure, to keep the stream short?
- The policy "needs 2 at 3+" is shown as amber in the progress line; should it be a link to the project policy for owners?
