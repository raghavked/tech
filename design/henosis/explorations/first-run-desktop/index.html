Spec

Window
960 × 724, not resizable during first run; side 312 navy, page cream. Traffic lights sit on the navy side.
Steps
Welcome · Connect to a server · Sign in · The tray. Steps 2 and 3 are the web client's step 1 split by what the shell must know first (setIdentity(serverUrl, userId)).
Stage marks
The four stages of the mark: a faint ring, one arc, two arcs apart, the closed ring with its centre. Done steps gain a green check; the current step sits on apricot wash.
Server
Defaults to the last remembered URL, then http://127.0.0.1:7700. Connect checks GET /api/me and shows version, team and projects.
Sign in
Name, user id (checked against users.json as you type), token optional and kept in the keychain when ticked. The role line reads from memberships.
Tray
Polls /api/notifications?unread=1 every 15 s and on focus. Rows: Pending approvals · N, Handoffs offered · N, Open, Quit. Windows carries the count in the tray; no badge.
Motion
Arcs-close on the welcome mark; the dot pops in on the tray icon; the badge pops. Calm mode and reduced motion turn all of it off.
