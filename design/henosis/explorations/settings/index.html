Team policy

What every new session is born withBilling page

Every new session in Billing page starts with a plan. The plan goes ahead when two contributors rate it 3 or more. Nothing irreversible happens until two contributors rate it 4 or more.

Plan first
Plan firstThe agent proposes steps, token estimates and risk before any work; until the plan is approved every call above read is refused
