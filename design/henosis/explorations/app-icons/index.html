Henosis · brand · exploration 03 app iconsone master, eleven cuts

The same ring on every door

One master artwork — two arcs, a person's and an agent's, closed into one ring around one centre — cut for macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, the favicon, the tray and Slack. The disc is navy in the stores, deeper navy in dark mode, cream on documents. The centre dot is the bead: it means joined, so the tray drops it when nothing is connected.

02

Grounds, light and dark, with and without the bead

Four grounds, one rule each. The bead is a state, not decoration.

Navy#2A3244

The store icon, the dock, the taskbar. Light-mode default.

Deep navy#0F131B

Dark-mode variant (--mark-disc in dark). iOS 18 dark icons, Android themed, macOS dark dock.

Cream#FBF7F1

Documents and exports only: the invoice PDF header, the Billing page's share card. Left arc turns navy so the ring holds.

MonochromecurrentColor

Tray template, Android themed foreground, print. One colour, alpha only, no disc.

With the beadjoined · live · default

Every store, dock, home-screen and Slack icon carries the bead. It is the product's promise: someone is in the circle.

Without the beadtray only · nothing connected

The empty ring is reserved for live status: the tray when you are signed out or no session is running, and the moment before a join animation. Never shipped as a static app icon.

04

Android layers and iOS appearances

The one place a launcher may crop us. The safe zone decides the mark's scale for the whole set.

Adaptive layers108 dp canvas · 72 dp visible · 66 dp safe

Ring outer edge at 54 dp, 12 dp inside the safe zone: no mask can touch the arcs. The ring is 75 % of the visible circle, a hair bolder than iOS on purpose, because launchers shrink icons to 48 dp.

Launcher maskscircle · squircle · rounded · teardrop · themed

Masks shown at the 72 dp visible region. The themed (Material You) icon is the monochrome layer on the wallpaper tint; here on apricot for the shot.

09

Don'ts

Six ways the set drifts, each seen in a real build somewhere.

✕

Disc inside a shell. Two circles, a target. The shell is the ground; the mark goes bare on it.

✕

One-colour ring. Without chocolate it is a spinner, and the older ring-and-bead the founder retired.

✕

Arcs swapped. The person's arc is on the left, apricot; the agent's is on the right, chocolate. Everywhere.

✕

Gradient disc. The brand gradient is for hero moments and the new-session button, never the icon.

✕

No bead on a store icon. An empty ring is a status, not an identity. Only the tray may show it.

✕

Wordmark in the icon. The name sits under the icon on every platform already; the icon is the ring alone.
