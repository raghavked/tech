Rules

what every empty state must and must not do
Say what will be here, and who puts it here.

“No sessions on Billing page yet” names the place; the line names the people. A newcomer learns the product's nouns from its empty pages before they see a full one.

One action, in the hand's colour.

Chocolate for the thing to do, ghost for the one alternative. Never two primaries, never a list of links. The new-session gradient appears only where the button is the real one.

Rows stay quiet, pages get the mark.

A section inside a busy page gets the one-line row with the 22px mark. A whole page that is empty gets the 56px mark, the sentence and the ghost. Never stack two page-size empties on one screen.

The ghost is honest.

Dashed, in ink-3, with content that would be true: the project's real name, the real policy numbers (two at 4+, 120k). It never looks clickable and never pretends to be data.

Searching is not empty.

“Nothing matches” and “Nothing here at this scope” keep the closed mark and no ghost; the page is full, the filter is narrow. Only a page with no history at all shows the open ring.

Close the ring when the first thing lands.

The arcs swing in and the centre appears, then the empty state crossfades to the first row. The same join motion as a member arriving: “Dee's first session opens.”
