05Dark mode mapping

The caveat: at night the hand and the pulse share a colour

In light mode the button is chocolate and the live dot is apricot, two jobs, two colours. In dark mode both are apricot. The rule that keeps them apart is shape: the hand is always a filled pill or a filled arc; the pulse is always a dot, a ring, a glow or a wash, never a filled block. --team-highlight stays apricot even when a lead picks a team accent, so the live dot never changes colour with the brand.

Alternative worth testing: a dark-mode hand of apricot 400 (#E7CEB5) on the button only, so the pulse stays one notch deeper than the hand.

06Rules that fall out of the table

Six rules, each one a row above.

Apricot is never text on cream

1.55 : 1. It is a ground, a dot or a glow. The one exception is italic Instrument Serif in the rail, where the ground is navy (7.7 : 1).
