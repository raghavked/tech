Henosis Design system

Data visualisation

Charts in Henosis answer a manager's question in a sentence, then prove it with as little ink as the data allows. The brand palette does not draw the data: two hues stepped into a chart band do, and the hand and the pulse keep their jobs.

01 · PALETTE

Four jobs, four rules

The chart palette is not the brand palette. Dress Blues and Chocolate are stepped lighter and more saturated into the chart band so they clear 3:1 on the surface and stay apart under every kind of colour vision. The raw brand hexes keep their roles: the hand for the one total, the pulse for washes and the live dot.

  • Categorical is identity. Four slots in a fixed order; a fifth folds into Other.
  • Sequential is magnitude. One hue, light to dark, five steps.
  • Diverging is polarity. Chocolate and navy around a neutral midpoint.
  • Status is state. Reserved; always with an icon and a word.

02 · MARKS

Thin marks, white doing the separating

Columns are capped at 24px and the slot's leftover is air. Segments and neighbours are parted by a 2px gap in the surface colour, never a stroke. Dots carry a 2px surface ring so they stay legible where they cross a line.

  • Column / bar ≤ 24px, 4px rounded data end, square at the baseline.
  • Line 2px, round joins. Dashed means projected.
  • Marker r 4, series fill, surface ring.
  • Area the series hue at 10%.
  • Hover the mark lifts 12% brighter; nothing else moves.

03 · AXES AND GRID

Recessive, honest, numeric

The grid is one hairline in --line, solid, never dashed. The baseline is the only axis stroke and it sits in --ink-3. Ticks are JetBrains Mono at 11px in the faint ink. The y axis starts at zero for magnitude; a line chart that indexes to a base says so in its title.

Spacing

Measured from the plot edge.

  • Y ticks 8px left of the plot, right-aligned, vertically centred on the line.
  • X ticks 16px below the baseline, centred on the slot; the month once, at the first day.
  • Head title and sentence on one line, legend at the right; 10px to the plot.
  • Direct label 8px above the cap, mono 11.5px, --ink-2.
  • Reference lines budget dotted 2·4 in --ink-2; the 80% hairline in --warn at 70%.
  • Height 250px on desktop, 200px on a phone; a 7-day viewBox on a phone, not a shrunken 14.
04 · TOOLTIPS AND HOVER

Values lead, labels follow

A line chart has a crosshair that snaps to the nearest day and one tooltip that lists every series there. A bar or cell is its own hit target and lifts when hovered. Keyboard focus shows the same readout. Every value in a tooltip is also on the page: a direct label or the table.

  • Key a short stroke for lines, a 10px box for bars.
  • Order the value in mono first, the series name second, the context line last.
  • Hit target the mark plus its gap, at least 24px.
  • Position 14px beside the crosshair, on the side with more room; never over a reference label.

05 · STAT TILES

The number first, the proof beside it

A tile is label, value, delta, trend. The value is Instrument Serif because it has a name's weight on this page; it is the one place serif meets numbers. The delta is signed, against a named period, coloured by direction and whether up is good. The sparkline is twelve points in the faint ink with only the current period in colour.

  • Hero one per view, on the brand gradient, 48px.
  • Meter under a tile when there is a budget; amber past 80%.
  • Loading shimmer in the value's place, label stays.
  • Empty a dash and a sentence, never 0.

Rules

  • Value serif 36px, proportional figures; the unit is 18px in the faint ink. Hero 48px on the gradient.
  • Delta 12.5px: the signed number bold, the period plain. Green when the direction is good, amber when it needs a person, red when it is over.
  • Label sentence case, 13px, no trailing colon; the scope after a middle dot.
  • Sparkline 12 points, 30px tall, no axes; the last segment and dot in the series colour or the hand.
  • Meter 6px, the track in --surface-3, the fill in the hand; amber past 80%, red past 100%; the percentage in the delta.
  • Four up on desktop with the hero spanning two; two up on a tablet; one up on a phone with the hero first.
06 · SPARKLINES

A word-sized chart

A sparkline shows shape, not value: no axes, no grid, no labels. The line is 1.5px in the faint ink; the current period is 2px in colour with a ringed dot. The number beside it is the only number. It lives in roster rows, rail cards and the token chip, always with the value to its right.

  • Sizes 110×26 in a row, 64×18 on a rail card, 56×16 in a chip.
  • Points twelve; fewer than five becomes dots.
  • Colour the series colour for a series, the hand for a total, apricot on the rail.

07 · EVERY CHART SHIPS WITH

The table and the texture

Hover and colour are the second and third ways to read a chart. The first is the sentence; the fourth is the table, always rendered under the chart, never behind a toggle. Texture is the backup for colour: one hatch at 45° and its 135° mirror, tone on tone, switched on by the team look, print or forced colours.
