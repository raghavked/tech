Loading and connecting

Four waits, four loaders, one line of copy each. Motion means joining or living, so every loader is a picture of something becoming one: the arcs close, three dots converge, two strands weave, a skeleton breathes while the words arrive. Nothing spins for its own sake.

1Say who and what, never "loading". The line names the people, the session or the project. "Loading…" is the fallback the copy table never reaches.
2One loader per screen. The launch has the mark, a session the orbit, a project the strand, an operation the shimmer. A screen never shows two of them.
3Show what is already in. The rail is live while a session joins; the direction is readable while rows arrive; finished steps stay checked while one runs.
