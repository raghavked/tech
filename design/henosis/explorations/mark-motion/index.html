The mark in motion

Two arcs, a person's and an agent's, swing in from apart and close into one ring; then the centre appears. It plays whenever something joins: a member, an agent, a session connecting. One set of keyframes from tokens.css, four homes: the loading screen, the joined-the-circle line, the splash and the rail brand on load.

Swing easing

A fast start and a long decelerating tail: most of the 70° is gone in the first 300 ms, so the closing reads as arrival, not as a spin. The overshoot is keyed (−4°, +2°), not sprung: the mark lands identically on every device and in every screenshot.

Rules

  • Motion means joining. The arcs move only when a member, an agent or a session connects. Never on hover, never as decoration.
  • One moving mark per screen. If the loader is already playing, a join line shows the closed ring and lets its avatar ripple instead.
  • Loop only while something is genuinely loading. Past 8 s the loader keeps looping but the weave bar gets a message: "Still folding 1 284 events".
  • Calm mode and prefers-reduced-motion: the ring is closed from the first frame, the centre present. Same markup, animation: none. The joined line still appears; it just does not move.
  • Small sizes: 24 px and up animate. At 20 and 16 the arcs are too few pixels to read a swing, so the closed mark fades in over 200 ms instead.
  • The arcs never change colour during the run, and the centre never appears before the ring is one. A ring with no centre is a loader; a centre with no ring is a bullet.

Joined the circle

A join is a line in the stream, not a modal. The 48 px mark plays once: the arcs close, the centre appears, and as it does the newcomer's avatar ripples into the stack. The line says who, how, and how many are here. An agent joining uses the same card with its name in the hand colour and its crew.
