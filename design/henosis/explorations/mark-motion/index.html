The mark in motion

The ring is the team. The gap at the top is the seat kept for whoever comes next. When something joins, a person, an agent, a session reconnecting, the bead travels round the ring and settles into the gap. One animation, three homes: the loading screen, the joined-the-circle moment and the splash.

Travel easing

A slow start so the bead is seen arriving, a long decelerating tail so the gap reads as a destination, not a stop. The overshoot is hand-keyed, not a spring: the mark must land identically on every device.

Rules

  • Motion means joining. The bead moves only when a member, an agent or a session connects. Never on hover, never as decoration.
  • One moving mark per screen. If a loader is already playing, a join notice shows the settled mark and lets its avatar ripple instead.
  • Loop only while something is genuinely loading. Past 8 s the loader keeps looping but the weave bar gets a message: "Still folding 1 284 events".
  • Calm mode and prefers-reduced-motion render the completed mark, no trail, no halo. The joined line still appears; it just does not animate.
  • Below 24 px the trail and halo are dropped: the bead simply fades in at the gap.

Joined the circle

A join is a line in the stream, not a modal. The 48 px mark plays once, the newcomer's avatar ripples into the stack, and the line says who, how, and how many are here. An agent joining uses the same card with its name in the hand colour and its crew.
