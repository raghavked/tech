Four rules for the bad moments

Every state below is one sentence of fact, one of reassurance, one of intent, and one action. The colour says how much it matters: grey for the network, amber for waiting, red only when a person said no or two branches disagree.

1

Say what happened

A fact with a time on it. Last seen 14:02. Never "something went wrong".

2

Say what still works

The stream is readable, the log is on the server, your draft is held. The page never goes blank.

3

Say what we are doing

Trying again in 4s. The agent read the denial. The branch keeps its claims until you resolve.

4

One action, in the hand

Retry, Steer, Resolve. Chocolate, at most one per state. The rest are plain links.

  • The connection line keeps its three parts (state, next attempt, Try now) and drops the long reassurance; the composer carries it instead.
