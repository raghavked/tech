Badges and status words

Five words say what an agent is doing, one apricot number says what waits for you, one pill says who is in the room, one says the plan is not approved yet, and a ring in serif names the crew. Each has four sizes and lives on cream and on navy. Nothing else in the product gets a badge.

Rules

What keeps the page calm. Anything that bends one of these needs a reason in its notes.

  1. 1Five words and no synonyms: Running, Awaiting approval, Blocked, Paused, Idle. "Awaiting" alone is allowed only at 390 wide.
  2. 2Colour lives in the dot, never in the word. Words are ink or ink-2; on navy, rail-fg or rail-muted.
  3. 3Only Running moves. Awaiting approval has a halo and holds still. Calm mode stills everything.
  4. 4Solid apricot means one thing: a count of what waits for you. It is absent at zero; never "0".
  5. 5A number counts things you can act on. Unread chats count; tokens, people and steps are text, not badges.
  6. 6Team is apricot on chocolate; Solo is an outline; Planning is apricot-soft. A session wears at most one of Solo or Team and may add Planning.
  7. 7Crews are names, so they are serif with a hollow ring; the ring is never filled, that is the mark's job.
  8. 8At every size the dot keeps its drawing: filled, filled with halo, hollow, hollow and faint, dashed. Nothing is only a colour.
