# The rail · notes

## The idea
The rail is the team's roster, not a menu: the things that wait for you (Inbox, Approvals) sit pinned under the one gradient on the screen, then every live agent appears as its person's card under the project, crews first in serif, solos after, each saying what it is on, who it is with and what it has spent, and the chats and memory of the same circle follow. Apricot is reserved for one meaning in the rail: a solid apricot count means something waits for you, a soft apricot wash means where you are, and the running dot is the only thing that moves. Collapsed to 72px the same order survives as icons and avatars with their status dot at the corner, so the fleet is still readable when the rail is out of the way.

## What to keep
- The order: brand and collapse toggle; New session (gradient, with its N key); Search (also the palette, ⌘K); Inbox and Approvals with counts; Agents by project with crews in serif and a Solo pill where there is no crew; Memory with its conflict count as a hint; Chats with unread per group and summed in the section; New group; the you row with serif name, role and what you are driving.
- One row, four states: rest in rail-muted; hover on rail-2 with the icon warmed to apricot; active on the apricot wash with a hairline inset; keyboard focus as the team glow. Counts are mono numerals, chocolate on apricot, 20px, popping once and still in calm mode.
- Agent cards: status dot (running pulses, awaiting is amber, blocked is red, paused is muted, done is apricot), name, Team or Solo pill, the italic serif "what it is on", the person with avatar, and spend in mono at the end of the line that warms past 80 % of the budget.
- Collapsed rail at 72px: mark, gradient plus, search, Inbox and Approvals with corner counts, the project initial in serif, one avatar per agent with the status dot at the corner and the active one ringed in apricot, Memory, Chats with its unread, you, expand chevron; hover shows the card as a navy tooltip.
- The account menu stays navy (rail-2) and rises from the three dots on the you row: theme segment, notifications, calm mode, Team look, shortcuts, sign out.
- Mobile: the rail is a sheet with a "who · where" line and a close button, the same content at touch sizes; the lists scroll, the you row stays put.

## Open questions
- With two or more projects the rail gets long fast. Should a project fold its agents when it is not the active project, keeping only its count and any awaiting dot?
- Memory sits between Agents and Chats as the app does today; it may read better as a fourth pinned item under Approvals once it carries a conflict count.
- Should the collapsed rail show agent avatars at all when a person drives two sessions (AN twice), or switch to project initials with a stacked count?
- The New session key hint ("N") competes with the decorative ring; the ring could go, or the hint could live only in the tooltip.
- Recents are not shown here; the app lists them under Chats. Do they belong in the rail or only in search and the palette?
