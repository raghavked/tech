The ring is the illustration.

Henosis does not need characters, hands or isometric desks. The mark already says the whole product: two arcs, a person's and an agent's, closing around one centre. Every spot illustration is that ring in a different moment: open, joining, whole, shared, handed over, waiting, stepped, branched, remembered, rippled, contested, spent. Twelve spots, four inks, one grammar.

01

Grammar

One box, one radius, one stroke, four inks. Everything a spot can do is a change of position, length or presence, never a change of shape.

  • Box96 units. The ring sits at the centre with radius 28 and stroke 10, round caps. Satellites may leave the box; nothing may leave the 46 safe circle by more than a stroke.
  • Four inksChocolate for the person's arc, navy for the agent's, apricot for what is live, a dashed ghost for what has not happened. Amber appears once, in Contention.
  • Arcs move, never bendA spot is the two arcs translated, rotated, dashed or shortened. No new curves except smaller rings of the same family and ripples concentric to the centre.
  • The dot is a promiseSolid apricot means something is here. Dashed means it is expected. Absent means the illustration is not about presence at all (Meter).
  • No faces, no fills, no shadowsNever fill the ring, never outline it, never add a hand or a screen. The ring is the person and the agent; drawing them again would be saying it twice.
  • One spot per screenAn empty state shows one spot at 96. A card shows one at 48. A row shows one at 24. Marketing may compose many rings, but still one composition per screen.

Marketing

Outside the app the same ring composes: many small rings, open and closing, converging on one. Arcs go apricot and cream on the brand gradient and on navy.

One team of people and agents.

A new engineer and a new agent join the same circle on the same day: the same groups and chats, the same plan-first manners, the same release gate held by the people who use the code.

Plan first

The agent proposes steps with token estimates before it touches anything. Ana rates the plan; the kernel approves it when the policy is met.

Release gates

Irreversible work waits for two of the people who consume it, rating it four or better. An unrated approve is a voice, not a vote.

Handoff with a brief

Bo takes the baton from Dee with a brief the system wrote: the situation, the open items, what changed since she was last here.

Only one motion

Open becomes Whole with the brand's arcs-close keyframes (1.8s, the same ones a joining member gets), and the first real row crossfades in beneath it. No other spot animates: Baton's bead does not run, Inbox's ripple does not grow. Calm mode and reduced motion leave every spot at its final frame, which is designed to be read still.

Don't recolour

No team accent inside a spot; the Team look editor changes the glow, not the arcs.

Don't close the gap

One continuous circle is the older ring-and-bead. The arcs stay two arcs.

Don't cut the caps

Round caps always. Flat ends read as a loading spinner.

Don't add a ground

No disc, blob or shadow behind a spot on cream. The navy disc belongs to the mark alone.
