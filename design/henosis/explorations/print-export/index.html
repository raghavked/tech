What the session looks like on paper

A session exported to PDF and the audit trail printed. Paper is one theme: white, ink, cream for the few things that need a ground. The rail, the composer, the buttons, the glows and every animation stay in the app; the names, the acts, the ratings, the ids and the hash go on the page.

Paper does not follow the theme.
White page, ink #1F2634
Cream only for a quoted human and a gate
One green: what the kernel decided
One amber: what was masked
No apricot glow, no gradient, no shadow on paper.

Rules for paper

the print stylesheet in nine decisions; this board itself prints as the two sheets.

What leaves the page

Rail, composer, topbar, buttons, stars you can click, budget meters, avatars, toasts, the apricot glow, every gradient and every animation. A printed page has nothing to press.

on screen: [Approve 4★] [Revise] [Reject]
on paper: Bo approved ★★★★★ 4 · 13:49

What stays

  • Every name in Instrument Serif, with the role as it was at that moment.
  • Every id, hash, token count and time in JetBrains Mono.
  • Quotes and notes as serif italic, in quotation marks.
  • The kernel's decisions in green, with the rule beside them.
  • Masked secrets stay masked, counted in amber.

The mark on paper

The cream variant by default: chocolate and navy arcs, apricot centre, on a cream disc. In one ink the agent's arc is dotted so the two arcs still read as two. Never the navy disc on white; it prints as a black coin.

colour
one ink
not on paper

Type on paper

Sizes in points, one serif, one sans, one mono. Body at 11pt reads at arm's length; mono runs a size smaller so an id never outweighs the sentence it sits in.

Title, serif24pt A name, serif13pt Body, sans11pt ev_0044 · a3f9c219.5pt Column label8.5pt

Running head and foot

Head: the mark, the wordmark, team · project · kind, then who exported it and when, the session id and the branch. Foot: the hash and the masked count, the title, the page of pages. The same on every page so a single sheet out of the stack still says where it came from.

sha256 9b1e…c47d · 1 secret masked   ·   title   ·   page 3 of 6

Page breaks

  • A plan card never splits; it moves whole to the next page with its ratings.
  • A release gate keeps its votes and its grant together.
  • A ledger row never splits; the header row repeats on every page.
  • A long tool result folds to one line and its exit; the full text is in the bundle, not on paper.
  • Human messages keep with the agent's first reply.

Links and references

A link prints its text, then its address in mono and brackets. Internal references (an event, a plan, an approval) print as their id only, since the id and the hash are enough to find them in the bundle.

Signature and verify line

Both documents end with the same two lines: the green verify line with the replay command, and the signature row with the key name and the event count. A reader with a terminal can check the paper; a reader without one can see that it was signed.

✓ Replays to the same hash. henosis replay ses_7f3a9c --verify

What is omitted says so

Where the export leaves something out, the page says it in one italic serif line where it would have been: team chat stays in the circle, a branch was not united, a secret was masked. A silence on paper would look like a gap in the record.
