Desktop tray

Henosis lives in the menu bar and the system tray as the same three questions: what awaits you, where were you, and how do you get back in one click. The native menu lists the actual items, not counts; the native notification says one sentence with the first two buttons; every row and every banner is a henosis:// link to the right session.

The rules

The tray does not try to be the inbox. It answers what awaits you and gets you back in; everything else happens in the window, which every row and every banner opens on the right session.

What the menu holds

  • Pending approvals and Handoffs offered list the items, newest first, five each; a sixth becomes "and 3 more… in the queue".
  • Mentions appear only when there are some, and never add to the badge; they clear when the room is read.
  • Quick open is the five sessions and rooms you were in last, by their names, plus Inbox, Approvals queue, New session.
  • Pause notifications for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you say; the icon dims and a moon sits in the corner. Approvals still badge.
  • A row has one line: the serif who, the verb, the what in muted, the age at the right. Native menus take no second line, so the where moves into the banner.
  • Empty: the menu says "Nothing awaits you, Ana." and keeps Quick open.

What rings and what counts

  • The badge (Dock, launcher, Windows taskbar button) counts approvals plus handoffs addressed to you. Nothing else.
  • A banner for every approval, handoff, blocked, contention and mention that is not on screen; done and plan only when you asked for them in Settings.
  • Decisions (approval, handoff) are alert-style on macOS and keep their buttons on Windows; they stay until answered. News slides away.
  • Sound once per decision, and only after a minute away from the window.
  • Same ref twice replaces the notification by tag; approving in the window withdraws it.
  • Items of the session on screen never notify; they are already in the stream in front of you.

Where a click goes

  • Every row and every banner body opens henosis://p/<project>/s/<session> and scrolls to the notice.
  • A mention opens the room at the message; a Quick open room opens it at the last read line.
  • Approve · 4 on a banner votes the default rating without opening the window; under a ratings rule it opens the notice instead.
  • Take it accepts the handoff and brings the window forward with the baton in your hand; Later leaves the row in the menu.
  • The tray's title click opens the first pending item; a plain Open Henosis restores the last window and route.
  • ⌥⇧H / Ctrl+Shift+H is a global shortcut that brings Henosis forward from anywhere.
