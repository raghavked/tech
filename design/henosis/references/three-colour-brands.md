# Three-colour brands: chocolate, apricot, navy

Reference notes for Henosis. Palette: Chocolate Fondant 19-1432 (#56352D), Apricot Illusion 14-1120 (#E2C4A6), Dress Blues 19-4024 (#2A3244) on a cream canvas; Fraunces for display, Inter for UI. Sources read October 2026.

## What the best work does

**Pantone's own cards already describe the trio.** Chocolate Fondant reads "powerful, warm, emotional, present" and is paired with cream white, navy (Pageant Blue, Ocean Cavern), taupe and warm beige (https://modeinfo.com/PANTONE-SWATCHES-CHIPS-SHEETS/PANTONE-SMART-Color-Swatch-Cards-TCX-Cotton/PANTONE-COTTON-SWATCH-CARD-19-1432-TCX-Chocolate-Fondant.html). Apricot Illusion is "warm, lively, friendly, active", a medium-lightness, soft-chroma orange paired with natural white, sand, camel, brown (Dachshund, Emperador) and denim blue (https://modeinfo.com/PANTONE-SWATCHES-CHIPS-SHEETS/PANTONE-SMART-Color-Swatch-Cards-TCX-Cotton/PANTONE-COTTON-SWATCH-CARD-14-1120-TCX-Apricot-Illusion.html). Dress Blues is "clear, calm, confident, modern", paired with Coconut Milk, Hemp and Moonstone, i.e. cream, flax and sand (https://modeinfo.com/PANTONE-SWATCHES-CHIPS-SHEETS/PANTONE-SMART-Color-Swatch-Cards-TCX-Cotton/PANTONE-COTTON-SWATCH-CARD-19-4024-TCX-Dress-Blues.html). Colorxs lists Peacoat #2D3956, almost exactly our navy, as Chocolate Fondant's split-complement (https://www.colorxs.com/palette/color/pantone-19-1432-tcx-chocolate-fondant), and Pantone gives Desert Palm and Fig as its analogues (https://www.pantone.com/color-finder/19-1432-tcx). The lesson: the two darks are a theory-correct pair, and every list puts cream or sand between them.

**The best brands ration the warm colour.** Pentagram's Peach Coffee Roasters "limited the use of the color peach", set labels in all-caps Gilroy with ample white space, and spent the palette on gingham printed inside bag gussets and on three-colour cups (https://www.pentagram.com/work/peach-coffee-roasters). Dixon Rye's 2025 refresh makes Cream and a deep Lagoon the neutral bases, keeps Hague navy "at the forefront", runs Hague-on-Lagoon tone-on-tone patterns under lids, and gives Cocoa brown its own bright accent as a duotone; headlines in an angular serif, long copy in a workhorse sans (https://russellshawdesign.com/dixon-rye-2025-brand-refresh-design). VELA's navy ground carries a pale gold that is "never used as a fill, always as an accent that catches the light", plus a 0.5pt horizon rule above the wordmark that recurs everywhere "just enough to become a signature" (https://contra.com/p/mgvbOvOL-vela-brand-identity-and-website-design).

**Brown plus cream can be a whole system.** Aesop is amber-brown glass, parchment label, near-black ink, rectangles only, and exactly two textures: faintly reflective glass against a "matte, slightly toothy" paper label (https://designbycurio.com/learn/australian-aesop-apothecary-amber-2010s). Mutlu Makarna keeps its navy "on a matte texture" for the premium read and switches to brown for the whole-wheat range (https://packagingoftheworld.com/2022/01/mutlu-makarna-packaging-designs-by-orhan-irmak-tasarim.html).

**Interiors give the proportions.** Veranda: pair chocolate with off-white, never stark white; camel over grey because grey "does not have enough oomph"; when two deep colours meet, work "a warm cream hue into the scheme" as a visual break; chocolate wants velvet's sheen (https://www.veranda.com/decorating-ideas/color-ideas/a74149523/colors-that-go-with-chocolate-brown/). Apartment Therapy's brown-and-peach room works because brown furniture and dark trim ground the peach so it reads "layered and grown-up", and the standing advice is to mix leather, velvet, wood and bronze within one palette (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/what-colors-go-with-brown-37507765). Homestyler: "navy serves as an anchoring color, adding weight to the room while showcasing peach accents", on slubbed linen and off-white textiles (https://www.homestyler.com/article/floorplanner/mastering-peach-interior-design). Awedeco gives 60-30-10 for peach rooms and caps accents at 10-15% (https://awedeco.com/colors-that-go-with-peach/). Home Stratosphere: one navy lacquer wall "multiplies light" while three off-white plaster walls stop the room collapsing; dark paint elsewhere should be matte to avoid hot spots (https://www.homestratosphere.com/charcoal-navy-brass-living-room-designs/).

**Fashion gives the order and the texture rule.** Navy "brings out chocolate-brown shoes" and small surface patterns should be treated "as nothing more than texture" (https://www.permanentstyle.com/2018/08/suit-style-5-selecting-colour-and-pattern.html). Rampley: "darker below, lighter above, brighter on top" (https://www.rampleyandco.com/blogs/the-journal/the-complete-guide-to-men-s-colour-pattern-and-texture-matching). Westwood Hart: brown in a flat shiny fabric "becomes a completely different colour"; flannel, suede and heavy linen catch light in the weave (https://westwoodhart.com/blogs/westwood-hart/how-to-wear-brown-for-men-style-guide-rules). Derek Guy: within a narrow range, vary "texture, fibre and sheen" instead of colour (https://www.mrporter.com/en-us/journal/fashion/derek-guy-colour-25029222).

**Dark treatments keep the hue.** Material's dark theme uses smoky grey, not black, and desaturates accents (https://design.google/library/material-design-dark-theme). Paletter: the dark canvas keeps the light canvas's warmth, ink goes off-white, and elevated surfaces get lighter, not darker (https://paletter.dev/blog/dark-mode-palette). Cinatra draws hairlines as low-alpha navy, "never grey, so the warmth of the page reads through" (https://docs.cinatra.ai/references/design/design-system.html).

**For Henosis.** Light: cream at 60, chocolate as ink and the matte dark at 30, apricot and navy sharing the 10, apricot as light and navy as the single committed action. Dark: a deepened chocolate canvas, apricot-tinted ink, navy as the lit surface. Never grey, never blue-black.

## Ten elements for Henosis

1. **Toothy cream stock.** `background:#F6EFE4` with an inline SVG `feTurbulence baseFrequency="0.85"` grain at 4% opacity. Every screen. One sheet of paper under everyone's words.

2. **One ink.** All body text, person or agent, is `color:#56352D`; hairlines are `rgba(86,53,45,.14)`, never grey. Transcript. Authorship never changes the colour of the words.

3. **Closing-ring presence.** Two SVG circles, apricot and navy, `pathLength="100"` with `stroke-dasharray` driven by active-people and active-agent counts; the ring closes when both are present. Session header. The brand mark is the live state of the room.

4. **Navy commits.** Exactly one filled control per screen: `background:#2A3244; color:#F6EFE4`; every other button is a ghost with a chocolate hairline. Compose bar and instruct sheet. People and agents commit through the same button.

5. **Apricot as light.** The message being written gets `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(226,196,166,.7), 0 0 28px rgba(226,196,166,.35)`, a `radial-gradient` wash beneath it, and no fill. Council room. Warmth marks attention, not identity.

6. **Lacquer card.** The focused card is `linear-gradient(180deg,#303A4E,#232A3A)` with `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.08)`; every other card is flat `#2A3244`. Fleet board. One shiny surface among matte ones, like the lacquer wall.

7. **Linen muted.** Secondary and status text use `color-mix(in oklch, #56352D 62%, #F6EFE4)` rather than any grey. Rail and directory. Agent status reads in the same linen as people's.

8. **Tone-on-tone owner strips.** Plan-card owner bands are `repeating-linear-gradient(135deg, var(--owner) 0 6px, color-mix(in oklch, var(--owner) 92%, white) 6px 12px)`, apricot family for people, navy family for agents. Plan cards. Difference is a weave, not a warning.

9. **Gingham empty state.** Two crossed `repeating-linear-gradient` layers, apricot and navy at 8% over cream, `background-blend-mode: multiply`. Empty council and new session. Two threads, one cloth.

10. **Deepened-chocolate dark mode.** `:root[data-theme="dark"]{--canvas:#1E1512;--surface:#2A1E1A;--ink:#EAD9C8;--commit:#2A3244}` with elevation stepping lighter. Every screen. Night keeps the warmth; nobody's words go cold.
