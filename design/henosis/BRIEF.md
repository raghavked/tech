# Henosis design brief

Henosis (ἕνωσις): union, becoming one. The product: one team of people and agents. Agents are
members, not tools. The selling moment is joining: a new engineer and a new agent enter the
same circle on the same day, with the same groups and chats, the same plan-first manners, the
same release gate held by the people who consume the code, and the same manager's view.

## Palette and roles
Dress Blues #2A3244 is the frame (the rail, titlebars, the dark canvas). Cream #FBF7F1 is the
page, with a faint apricot wash. Chocolate Fondant #56352D is the hand: every primary action.
Apricot Illusion #E2C4A6 is the pulse: live things, glows, the centre dot, selection, team messages.
Gradients navy→chocolate for hero moments and the new-session button. Soft shadows are allowed.
Vibrant, expressive, native, original, professional. Never garish: one gradient per screen.

## The mark
Two arcs, a person's and an agent's, that close into one ring around one centre (design/mark.svg):
the right arc is chocolate, the left arc apricot, the centre dot apricot, on a navy disc; on cream
the arcs are chocolate and navy with an apricot centre. Motion: the two arcs swing in from apart and
close into the ring, then the centre dot appears, whenever something joins (a member, an agent, a
session connecting). The founder chose this mark; do not draw the older ring-and-bead.

## Type
Instrument Serif for everything that has a name (titles, people, agents, crews, groups).
Instrument Sans for everything that is read. JetBrains Mono inside tool lines and numbers.

## Motion principles
Motion means joining or living. Loaders: arcs-close (the mark), orbit-converge (three dots in the three
colours become one), weave (two strands), shimmer (skeleton). Side motion: cards rise in,
status dots pulse, badges pop, a joined avatar ripples, a granted approval draws its check,
the Team pill crossfades, toasts rise. Calm mode and reduced motion turn all of it off.

## What the app has (design every one of these as a professional, finished screen)
Rail with agent cards (status, what it is on, Solo/Team, who with, crews, tokens); session
stream with human, agent and team messages, tool steps, approvals with 1–5 ratings, release
gate notices, plan cards (steps, token estimates, ratings, approve/revise), contention and
handoff notices; composer with Agent/Team toggle; Team panel (people, crew, chat); Details
drawer (intent, branches, workspace, usage, memory, brief); groups and chats with people and
agents as members and @mentions; project page (direction, agents by crew, contentions, team
chat, memory); manager overview (sentence summary, needs-you, tokens by project/person/agent,
plans rated and approved, release gates, crews, groups, members and agents roster); token
usage meters and budgets; token optimisation (plan estimate vs actual, savings); inbox;
approvals queue; memory browser; replay scrubber; branch compare and unite; settings with the
Team look editor (accent, mark, motion, emblem); command palette; shortcuts sheet; search;
share and invite; export; onboarding "join the circle"; desktop window with tray; mobile
session, chat, approvals, rail; dark mode.

## Voice
Calm, specific, warm. "Ana joins the circle." "Bo has the baton." "United pdf-layout into
main." "The plan waits for two ratings." "Cy's agent answered in #billing." Never "AI-powered".

## Rules for an exploration
Self-contained index.html using ../../tokens.css plus a page <style>; real content (Ana, Bo,
Cy, Dee; Billing page, Checkout; invoice PDF, tax lines, proration); desktop 1440×900 unless
the topic is mobile (390×844) or a brand board; light and dark both correct; screenshots
taken and looked at; no external scripts; notes.md with the idea, what to keep, open questions.
