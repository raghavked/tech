Henosis

ἕνωσις · union, becoming one. Two arcs, a person's and an agent's, close into one ring around one centre.

The mark

The chocolate arc is the person, the apricot arc the agent; neither is complete alone. The centre is the work. Clear space: one arc width on every side.
