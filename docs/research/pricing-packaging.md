# Pricing and packaging for Henosis

Research memo, 2026-10-02. Topic: per seat vs per session-hour vs per approver for a desktop-first team agent product; how Linear, Figma, Cursor, Devin and Claude Team price in 2026; procurement expectations; recommended Henosis tiers.

## Why it matters for Henosis

Henosis's premise is that anyone on the team can open the session, watch, and steer. Classic per-seat pricing taxes exactly that behaviour: every observer becomes a line item, so teams ration access and the product collapses back to one person babysitting an agent. `docs/08_business_model.md` already states the hypothesis (active session-hours plus approver seats), but nothing in `packages/protocol`, `packages/server/src/orgs.ts` or the clients models a plan, a billable seat or a metered hour. The kernel already has the two facts a meter needs: a `Role` lattice (observer < contributor < driver < owner) and a `RiskClass` on every tool call (`packages/protocol/src/index.ts`). The question is whether the 2026 market supports charging on those axes, and what procurement will insist on.

## Prior art

1. **Claude Team and Enterprise (claude.com/pricing, fetched 2026-10-02).** Team: standard seat $20/month annual ($25 monthly), premium seat $100 annual ($125 monthly), 2 to 150 seats, seats mixable, "extra usage" switchable on, SSO and central billing included. Enterprise: "$20/seat/month billed annually" plus usage at API rates, SCIM, audit logs, compliance API, custom retention. The pattern: a cheap identity seat for everyone, a premium seat for heavy agent users, metered usage in dollars on top.
2. **Figma (figma.com/pricing and pricing FAQ via Exa, accessed 2026-10-02).** Four seat types. View seat free. Collab $3/$5/$5, Dev $12/$25/$35, Full $16/$55/$90 per month on Professional/Organization/Enterprise; Organization and Enterprise annual only; admins must approve new paid seats, which are prorated and invoiced quarterly. Each seat carries a monthly AI credit allotment. This is the closest analogue to Henosis: price the person by what they may *do*, not by whether they may look.
3. **Linear (linear.app/pricing and billing docs via Exa, accessed 2026-10-02).** Free with unlimited members, 2 teams, 250 issues; Basic $10/user/month annual; Business $16; Enterprise custom, annual only, with SAML/SCIM, invoice/PO billing, audit log. Guests exist only on Business and are billed as full members. Secondary commentary (issuelinker.com, 2026-08-13) calls the absent read-only seat "the single most expensive assumption teams make." A cautionary tale for a product whose value is in observers.
4. **Cursor (cursor.com blocked; agentdeals.dev, vibecompare.dev, bestagenthub.com comparison pages, accessed 2026-10-02; UNVERIFIED on the vendor page).** Hobby free, Pro $20, Pro+ $60, Ultra $200 per month for individuals; Teams Standard seat $40 ($32 annual) and Premium seat $120 ($96 annual, 5x usage) since the June 2026 restructure; Enterprise custom with pooled usage, invoice/PO billing, SCIM, audit logs. Flat-rate "Auto" mode ended 2026-08-24; usage now bills at the routed model's list price.
5. **Devin (devin.ai and docs.devin.ai blocked; stackaible.com 2026-07-07, agentdeals.dev, bestagenthub.com 2026-09-07; UNVERIFIED).** In 2026 Cognition retired the $20 + $2.25/ACU Core plan and the $500/month Team plan (250 ACUs included) in favour of Free, Pro $20, Max $200, Teams "$80/month base + $40 per full dev seat", Enterprise custom still metered in ACUs (one ACU is roughly 15 minutes of active agent work). Secondary sources disagree on the Teams formula ($80 + seats vs an $80 minimum), which is itself a lesson: a formula that two reviewers read differently will be read differently by procurement too.
6. **Anthropic Managed Agents pricing (platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/pricing returned 404; UNVERIFIED).** `docs/08_business_model.md` cites it as the reference for billing active runtime and not idle. Treat the per-active-hour anchor as a hypothesis until re-fetched.

## What to borrow

- **Free viewers, paid authority (Figma, Claude).** Figma's free View seat and Claude's $20 standard seat both make it costless to add the people who only need to see. Henosis's observer and contributor roles should be free and unlimited; driver and owner are the billable seat. This matches `docs/08` and matches how organisations count authority.
- **Two seat weights, not ten (Claude, Cursor).** Both vendors converged on a standard seat and a premium seat at roughly 5x the price with 5x the usage. Henosis can do the same with pooled active hours per approver seat.
- **Usage in dollars with a visible pool (Cursor, Devin's 2026 self-serve plans).** Devin moved its self-serve plans from ACUs to dollar-denominated usage and kept ACUs only for Enterprise. Opaque units are a procurement objection. Henosis should meter active session-hours but display and invoice them in dollars with a pooled allowance.
- **Admin approval of new paid seats (Figma).** Promotion to driver or owner in Henosis already produces a `role.changed` event; when it would create a new paid seat, an org admin should approve it, prorated to the term.
- **Annual-only above the self-serve tier with invoice/PO, SSO, SCIM, audit log (Linear, Figma, Cursor, Claude).** Every vendor sells the same procurement bundle; SSO has moved down to the mid tier (Claude Team includes it), while SCIM, audit export and custom retention stay at Enterprise.

## What is unsolved

- **What is an "active" hour for one agent with many humans?** Devin's ACU counts VM time plus inference. Henosis's agent spends much of its wall clock waiting on approvals, quorum and contentions. Billing wall clock would punish governance; billing only model time ignores runner cost. Nobody prices the wait state.
- **Who pays when a session is forked.** A fork at a checkpoint doubles active hours while a team explores two branches. No comparator has a branch primitive.
- **Fleet-level and memory-level value.** Claims, lead directives, fleet briefs and organisation memory (`packages/fleet`, `packages/memory`) are consumed by managers who may never hold a driver seat. Figma charges Collab seats for exactly this audience; whether a "lead seat" is a third weight or a Business-tier feature is untested.
- **BYO harness and BYO model key.** Henosis hosts the session, not the model. The per-hour anchor from Devin ($9/hour of ACUs including inference) does not transfer; Henosis's hosted-runner cost per active hour is far lower and its value is in the record.

## Concrete recommendations for Henosis

1. **Tiers.** *Free*: unlimited observers and contributors, up to 3 approver seats, 1 project, 20 pooled active hours/month, 30-day log retention, BYO key. *Team*: $30 per approver seat/month annual ($36 monthly), 40 pooled active hours per seat, overage $0.75 per active hour billed in dollars, SSO, Slack adapter, 1-year retention. *Business*: $75 per approver seat, 200 pooled hours per seat, fleet claims and lead directives, fleet brief, memory curator, audit export, 3-year retention. *Enterprise*: annual only, invoice/PO, SCIM, compliance API, self-hosted runner, custom retention, Merkle-proof export (`docs/research/merkle-proofs-observers.md`), plus a platform fee per harness adapter. Replace the "Pricing (working hypothesis)" section of `docs/08_business_model.md` with this table. A 40-engineer team with 10 approvers and 200 hours lands at $300 to $750/month, within the "low thousands" envelope the doc already assumes.
2. **Define the billable seat in the protocol.** Add `Plan`, `SeatClass` (`approver`) and a `seat.granted` / `seat.released` event pair to `packages/protocol/src/index.ts`, derived from `role.changed` when the new role is driver or owner. Observers and contributors never emit a seat event.
3. **Meter active hours from the log, not the clock.** Add `packages/server/src/metering.ts` that folds each session's events into active intervals: agent turn start to turn end, excluding spans where the session is blocked on `approval.requested`, a quorum, or a contention with no driver. Emit `usage.metered` hourly into the org log in `packages/server/src/orgs.ts`. Forked branches meter independently; the merge event closes the branch's meter.
4. **Admin approval for new paid seats and spend caps.** In `packages/server/src/orgs.ts`, a role promotion that would exceed the plan's seat count waits on an org admin; a project-level monthly cap on active hours pauses the agent with a quiet notice in the conversation column (`apps/web`, composer notice, one line, no modal) rather than failing the tool call.
5. **Show plan state where the desktop lives.** In `apps/desktop` and `apps/web` the sidebar settings panel shows seats used, pooled hours remaining and the next invoice, in the same quiet register as other notices; the Tauri shell keeps a signed entitlement cache so an expired token degrades to observer rather than locking the window.
6. **Reprice the product spec's roles table.** `docs/03_product_spec.md` should state, per role, whether it is free or an approver seat, and `docs/07_security_and_compliance.md` should list the Enterprise procurement bundle (SCIM, audit export, retention, self-host) so sales conversations have one source.
